## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A short label next to a divider line goes to the wrong place** (e4, e5): "March 2027" typed above the
  header divider bound to the line and was built centred and pushed down; "Cloudbox" bound to the header line
  and landed *inside* the search box. The note was 35px above the line and left of any box.
- **A note on a short underline is not sized as a heading** (e5): "My files" came out small grey body text,
  though the line sits alone where a heading goes. (e1/e2 headings with "bold" in the note were fine.)
- **A colour word in a note leaks to its box** (e2): "12 blue bars" painted the chart panel a blue gradient.
- **A drawn table box lost its border** (e2): the rows box was built bare (the n15 "bare rows" rule fires on
  a box that is a card in the screenshot).
- **Round-ish avatar marks were built as hexagons** (e3): the drawn outline is pasted as the shape instead of
  snapping to a circle (same family as the c5 hexagon pill).
- **"Name: message" notes are built on one line** (e3); the screenshot has the name above the message.
  Invented a "DIRECT MESSAGES" heading nobody drew.
- **Day labels were put inside the drawn grid box** (e4), pushing the hour grid down by one row.
- **A page tap colours the header strip too** (e5): the header should stay white above its divider.
- **Many taps for many colours** (e3): 10 of 22 strokes were colour taps (4 rail squares, 3 avatars, 3 areas).
  A way to colour a group with one tap would halve this.
- **A note beside the end of a line binds to the box above, not the line** (e1): had to drag it on.
- **Builds are slow on table pages** (e5: 343 s, 114k tokens for a 6-row table).
