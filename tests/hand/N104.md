# N104: position words in a note are never printed; a sentence after a semicolon is copy

Gap (n103, round 37 team chat): the sketcher typed "Mia · 2:14 pm above; Copy is final. Can someone check the
pricing page on mobile?" on a message box: a small label, where it goes ("above"), then the message. The tool split
every note at the semicolon into copy and directions, so it printed "2:14 pm above" as a label and treated the
message itself as a direction (its own check then flagged the message as "a direction printed on the page"). The
dotted label was also read as a row of data ("Mia" and "2:14 pm" as two cells to keep apart).

## What was built
- **Position words** (`typedcopy.ts noteParts`): a trailing run of position words (above, below, under, beside,
  on top...) on a label that has a time, number or separator, or that is followed by a message, is where the label
  goes, never copy. Look words in that run ("small grey above") go to the look. Plain copy that ends in such a word
  ("Rise above") is left alone.
- **Sentences after a semicolon are copy**: a part of four or more words that reads as a sentence (ends like one, or
  starts with a capital that is not a direction word) is the item's own text; short state parts ("done M T W F",
  "image in grey bubble") stay directions.
- The build is told: the label text, where it goes, and the message in full. The page is checked that "pm above"
  (the last words of the label plus the position word) is never printed, even when the name sits in its own span.
- A dotted label placed by a position word is no row of data (`rowdata.ts`).
- **Drawn width** (`drawnorder.ts`): a stack of drawn boxes (a chat thread) is told each box's drawn x and width, so a
  bubble is as wide as drawn; the check fails a bubble built under three quarters of its drawn width whose words wrap.
