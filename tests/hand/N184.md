# n184: a ring in a coloured pen is a flat disc in that colour; an outline stamp stays a hollow ring

Found in n175 and n177 (round 43 c5 climbing gym, c4 letterpress).

## What was built
- **A ring inside a depiction stays a ring.** When the reading called a group of marks a picture of something
  ("climbing holds", "a round stamp"), every ring in it was traced as a path, so the pen colours and the drawn
  spots were lost: the gym's orange, yellow and teal holds came out black, orange and teal at random places, and
  the ink check then asked the letterpress stamp to be *filled* dark (a dark blob). `intent2.ts`: a ring drawn in a
  coloured pen with no tap, or a ring whose own note calls it an outline, a stamp's ring, a track or an arc, is kept
  as a ring mark even inside a depiction. The rest of the depiction is traced as before.
- **Then the existing rules apply.** A coloured-pen ring is a flat disc in exactly its pen colour where drawn (checked
  on the page); a ring noted "outline" is a hollow ring in its pen colour (n129, checked: no filled disc).
- `hollowring.ts` `colouredPen`: a pen with a clear hue (the gap between its strongest and weakest channel is 60 or
  more); black, grey, white and dark brown writing pens are not.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n184-pen-rings.test.ts` (5), using hand rings walked and wobbled the way the
  session draws them.
