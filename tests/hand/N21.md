# n21: words sit where they were drawn or typed (goal 2026-10-27)

## Built this step
- **A word on a drawn line keeps the line's side.** A level line with its own note
  ("Prism 2" on a line 28px in from the hero's left edge, 440px short of its right)
  is given its side of the box around it (left, centre or right). Codegen is told
  the side and the x the first letter starts at, and the page is checked on the
  letters themselves (not the block): left words must start within 24px of the
  line's start (`textplace.ts`: `markTextPlaces`, `textPlaceBrief`, `verifyTextPlace`).
  A note on a lone line (no focus, its cluster is that one line) now counts as its own.
- **A note typed at a spot in a big box keeps its spot.** Every note now carries
  where it was typed. A note bound to one big box is told to codegen with its spot
  ("lower left part of the box, about 65% down"): copy sits there, a description of
  the box has no spot. When the built page carries the note's words, they must sit
  within 60px (or a fifth of the box) of that height.
- **A first build that thinks long is not killed.** Found on the way: the bento
  build was stopped twice by the watchdog after 150 s of thinking (n22 made
  thinking "no progress" for every turn), so the page failed with nothing built.
  Codegen now streams partial messages; a build counts any streamed delta as life,
  a repair still needs tool calls or words and an edit within 180 s.
- **A button in a card follows its card, not the box in the row above.** The button
  in the CTA card was told it "only follows" the quote card above-left of it (outside
  its card), so three repair rounds never moved it.
- **Text on a button is judged on the button.** The contrast check ranked a positioned
  card above the static button inside it, read "Get Prism 2" as indigo on indigo, and the
  repair made it white on a white button (invisible). A box now paints over its
  ancestors' fills.
- The hand session records each note's spot in the digest and stops waiting when a run fails.
