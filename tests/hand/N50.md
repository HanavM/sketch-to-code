## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **Undo takes back the last action, whatever it was, and there is no way to rub out one chosen mark**
  (e2): I wanted to replace a hexagon-ish play circle; undo first removed a colour tap, then two more
  tile taps, and the hexagon stayed. Needs an eraser (scrub over a mark) and undo that says what it took back.
- **A coloured dot tapped inside a box refills the whole box instead of placing a dot** (e4): four red pin
  taps on the green map turned the map red; I had to tap green again. Then the build ignored where the
  dots were: pins landed at invented spots, and a fix "put the pins on my red dots" moved none of them.
  A tap in a box should be a fill only when it is the first/only colour; a small dot of a second colour
  is a placed mark (pin, status dot) at that spot.
- **A big box around everything is read as an area, not a card** (e3): the white invoice card was not built
  (all grey page), and the heading note bound to the box built a giant "1042" filling it (the n17 big-word rule
  misfired on a heading note). Two fixes: the first "white card" note went to the 1042 element and made a
  white box at the bottom; a whole-sketch note finally made the card.
- **Drawn edges drift by 15-25px** (e1, e5): the footer drawn at 588 built at 606, cards drawn 200-1080 built
  216-1063, so layout fell to 71-73% on pages that look right. e5 lost 14 points to this alone.
- **The page tap is not shown on the sketch** (e1, e2, e3; known from n49).
- **The toolbar hides drawing under it** (e2, e4): the progress line under the bar was invisible while drawing.
- **Invented extras**: nav icons (e2), gradients on flat tiles (e2, e4 map), shadowed thumbs (e4).
- **Words run high on content-heavy pages** (e2, e3, e4: 68-74): every label has to be typed; the word counter
  also counts "★", "·" and "/" as words.
