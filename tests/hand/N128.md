# n128: a closed stroke with 5-7 clear corners is that polygon; a coloured-pen polygon is filled in its pen colour

Found in n126 (round 39 c1 honey co.): eight hexagons, each one closed stroke of six straight sides in its own
pen colour, were read as rings and built as ovals (twice), and came out solid black on the first build.

## What was built
- **Polygons** (`shapemark.ts` `polygonSides`): a closed stroke at least 60px each way, no more than twice as wide
  as tall, that simplifies (1.5% of its diagonal) to 5-7 corners, each a turn of 30-110 degrees, every side a real
  share of the outline, and the ink within that tolerance of the straight sides all the way round, is a polygon,
  not a ring. A corner drawn a little round (two points close together) counts as one corner. It is then built
  clipped to its drawn outline (the n51 outline path), and told by name (pentagon / hexagon).
- Kept as before: 8-move rings (how dozens of rings in rounds 31-39 were drawn), smooth rings, small six-move
  avatars, wide pointed pills. Checked over every stroke drawn in rounds 31-39 (1,569 strokes, 5 hand wobbles
  each): only the eight honey hexagons change reading.
- **Pen colour fills** (`intent2.ts`, `outline.ts`): a polygon drawn in a coloured pen with no tap is told
  "background <pen colour>, never black", light pens included (the light-pen frame rules for boxes no longer
  catch polygons), unless its own note says outline / hollow / empty.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 5 unit tests.
