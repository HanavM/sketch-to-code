# n151: a lone slanted pen line is a thin line, never a filled box

Found in n149 (round 41 c1, kite club): each kite string, one slanted navy stroke, was built as a wide navy rectangle
(the line's bounding box filled); a fix note "strings: thin slanted lines, not bars" made them right. Seen again in
both n204 tries of round 45 c4 (bike repair): the crossbar between the wheels, one slanted black stroke, came out a
black box over the bike.

## Why it happened
- Inside a depicted picture (the reader called it "a bicycle", "kites") a stroke is not a shape mark, so its pen
  colour fell through to the catch-all: "the outline drawn there is filled with the pen colour", on its bounding box.
  For a slanted line that box is a 236x78 slab. The tool itself asked for the box.
- Outside a picture, a line's colour was told as "that text or rule is coloured ...", which says nothing about the slant.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A slanted line is a line** (`slantline.ts`, `slantOf`): an open stroke at least 40px long, straight (every point
  within about 6% of the chord), not level and not plumb (10 to 80 degrees), drawn in a pen with no tap, not a word
  line, not a route, and not a page-spanning colour edge. Inside a depicted picture too.
- **Thickness**: a neutral outline ring it touches sets it (a bike's frame matches its wheels); "thin" makes it 2px,
  "thick" 10px; otherwise 3px.
- **Told plainly**: a thin straight line from the drawn start to the drawn end at that angle, as a rotated div (exact
  left, top, width, height, transform-origin, rotate) or an SVG line; never a filled box over its bounding box.
- **Checked on the page** (`slantBuilt`): an unrotated block (or svg rect / polygon) in the pen colour covering most of
  its bounding box fails, with the exact rotated div to build instead.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n151-slant-lines.test.ts` (4).

## Did it help
- **The gap is fixed**: the kite strings and the bike crossbar come out as slanted lines on the first build, no fix
  note (kite: one fix note and 6 words saved, about 100k fewer tokens and 3 minutes faster).
- **Likeness did not rise**: the strings and crossbar are small marks. The kite's first build lost layout to a
  headline built low (the known "stacked heading sits low" gap), and the bike's service cards came out shorter and lower than drawn on both
  tries (82.8, 77.1 against 85.9, 88.2 in n204). The change does not touch the cards, but two low tries in a row is
  worth a look: split out as its own node (n207) to replay the bike again and find why the cards shrink.
- Not done in this step (left open in n151's place, split out): a short line under a picture card is its words
  (plant shop name and price), now its own node (n206).
