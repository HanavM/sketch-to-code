# n144: a pale divider colours only its rule; a list note stays where typed; picked rows are outlines; rows split name | details | price

Found in n139 / n142 (round 40 e2 recipe, e3 ride booking).

## What was built
- **A pale-pen divider colours only the rule** (`ruleline.ts` `paleOn`, `intent2.ts`, `inkcolour.ts`): a divider with words
  typed over it (n142's rule label), drawn in a pen that barely shows on the page (contrast under 1.8:1 against the tapped
  page colour), gives that colour to the rule only. The words over it are ordinary dark text. Told in the brief and checked on
  the page (`pale divider`: no text over the rule painted within ΔE 12 of the pen). A dark pen, or a white pen on a dark page,
  still colours the words as before.
- **A list note never joins a drawn mark through its own "both"** (`@s2c/ink` `notefocus.ts` `listNote`): a note listing three
  or more items after a colon is copy, so its "both" / "every" is about the items, not about drawn marks. Before, "checkbox
  list: ..., 2 eggs (both ticked orange ...)" typed in empty space 120px right of the photo box counted as a group note and was
  pulled into the photo box. Now it is "placed here".
- **A dark-pen box with no tap is an outline when its twin was tapped** (`inkcolour.ts` `inkTaughtOutline`): if the person
  tapped another box drawn in the same dark pen (they fill by tapping), a box in that pen with no tap, on a light surface, at
  least 64x24, holding no other boxes and with no note asking for a fill, is a 2px outline with a light inside (the picked
  row). Before, the picked "Standard" ride row was a near-black slab.
- **Rows typed with semicolons, name and price run into the details** (`rowdata.ts` `semiRows`, `splitEnds`): "label: row ·
  row; row · row" fills one row per semicolon part (the words before the colon say how they look), and a dotted row typed
  as "Standard 4 min away · 4 seats £18.40" is name | "4 min away · 4 seats" (one details line, dots kept) | "£18.40". Only
  a row ending in a price is split this way ("Sertraline 50 mg · 1 tablet daily" keeps its cells). The rows brief now puts
  details on a second line under the name when the drawn row is tall enough.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 9 unit tests (`packages/pipeline/tests/n144-pale-rule-rows.test.ts`).
