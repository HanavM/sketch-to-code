# n82: a box with a number and its label is a stat card

Round 35 e2 (library loans) and e3 (workout log), n77: boxes typed "6 big, on loan grey" (library) or
"Total lifted, grey" + "12480 kg, huge bold white" (workout). "big"/"huge" plus a number made each box an AREA
(n14: a box round a big word only marks where it sits), so the boxes were dropped and the numbers came out as
giant loose digits, centred. n77 needed a fix note on both pages ("each stat a white bordered card, number medium
not huge"; "two dark grey cards, numbers big but fit inside, left aligned").

## What was built (`statcard.ts`, zero tokens)
- A box (up to about 640x320, no drawn parts inside) whose notes give a number (with a short unit: 12480 kg,
  9 days, 62%) AND label words, in the same note or in another note on the same box, is a stat card, never an
  area. Look words (big, huge, grey, bold, white) are never the label; "on loan" keeps its "on".
- A box whose only words are a big number and look words ("huge outlined 09, white") stays an area; rows notes
  ("3 rows: ...", "6 rows like this", " / ") are never stats.
- Told: kept as a card at the drawn edges (a step off the page colour: white on a light page, dark grey on a
  dark one, or its tapped colour), the value big and bold but fitting, LEFT-aligned, the label small and grey
  under it (above when typed above), nothing else in it.
- Checked on the page (`stats`): the value inside the card at its left, the label inside on its typed side.
