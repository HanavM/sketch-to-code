# n224: a figure drawn in one stroke keeps its outline; bubbles stay rings

Found in n221 (round 47 c2, aquarium). Two fish drawn body and tail in one stroke came out as coloured rectangles. Of
the two small rings drawn, one became a filled dot and the other a tilted square. A fix note rebuilt the fish badly and removed both
rings. n223 took the fix note away, so the fish stayed boxes.

## Causes found
- **A closed outline with more than 8 corners was thrown out** as "not a polygon", so the fish had no outline to keep.
- **A ring drawn in eight moves passed as a turned square:** seen at a 22.5 degree turn, it fits a square tightly enough.
- **A note can only sit on one ring,** so "small outline bubbles" reached one of the two. The other followed the older
  rule that a coloured pen with no tap is a disc.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Drawn figures:** a closed stroke of up to 20 corners, at least 3 of them hard (60 degrees or more), is built clipped
  to what was drawn, unless the ink goes over itself like a scribble. Soft blobs never take this path.
- **Gentle corners are rounded off, hard ones stay put:** a fish's back is smooth, and its tail tips and waist are exactly
  where they were drawn. A skyline's steps stay square.
- **A ring is not a turned square:** a shape about as far from its centre all the way round stays a ring. A square really
  drawn turned still is one.
- **A note about the round things goes for all of them:** "outline bubbles", "hollow rings", "empty circles" apply to every
  untapped ring up to 120px on the page. "Outlined buttons" and plain "green dots" don't.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n224-drawn-figure.test.ts` (11). Demo: `out-n224/n224-demo.gif` (target, sketch, before, after).

## How far it reaches
Scanned every kept sketch (180 pages): only 3 strokes read differently as a figure, the two fish and the night skyline.
So this is a narrow change: it rarely fires, and these replays are nearly all the evidence available.

## Did it help
- **Fish were right in 2 builds of 2, with no fix note, and rings were rings.** Aquarium holds 82 at 43 words, where it
  took 66 words and a fix to reach 82.6.
- **The likeness gain is small:** about 1.5 points over n223. The score's layout part stays near 50 on this page, so a
  page can look much more right without scoring much higher. The fair gain is in what the page shows.
- **Skyline up 3.5 points on one run each.** Builds vary from run to run, so don't read much into that.
- These pages have been seen before, so none of this counts toward done.

## Not done, or still off
- **The title also asked that a fix on a shape never removes it. That was not built.** No fix was needed in these runs.
- **The target has four bubbles and this sketch drew two,** so the page has two. That is the sketch, not the tool.
- **The tapped pink oval came out a pill in both builds,** though told border-radius 50%. New node.
- The skyline's windows came out scattered and tiny. They were asked for only in a note, which gives no spots or size. Left alone.
