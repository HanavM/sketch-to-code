# n61: words on lines inside a box drawn turned sit on those lines

Replayed the n49 sketch of round 33 c3 (gig ticket: 14 strokes, 35 words, 8 notes) word for word with everything
through n60 plus this step: first build only, no fix notes, no note drags.

## The gap (from n59)
The ticket is drawn turned about 6 degrees and built turned, but the heading, the date and the stub words, each
typed on a line drawn inside it, landed 20-40px below their lines. Nothing told the build where those lines are in
the ticket's own frame, and nothing checked it. Found this step: in the real flow each of those notes is attached
to several marks at once (the line, the ticket, the next line), and the reading often puts the ticket and its lines
in different actions, so no line even carried its words.

## What was built
- **Lines inside a turned box are turned back into the box's own frame** (`tiltwords.ts`): each line drawn inside a
  box drawn turned is measured from the box's top-left corner before turning. The build is told, per line, where the
  letters sit (baseline on the line, first letter where the line starts), that a bare line under a heading is its
  next line, and to place the words in the box's own coordinates so the turn carries them onto the drawn lines.
- **A note typed on such a line is that line's words** (`bindTiltNotes`), even when it is attached to several marks
  and even across actions: a note typed just above or on a line, near where it starts, binds to its nearest line.
- **Checked on the page** (`verifyTiltWords`): the built words are turned back about the built ticket; their first
  line's baseline must be within about half a line of the drawn line (and start near where the line starts). A miss
  says how far up or down to move them inside the turned box.
