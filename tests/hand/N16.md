# n16: words keep their look and read on what is behind them (goal 2026-10-27)

What was built (branch try/n2-shape-vocab, on top of n15):

- **An outlined big word stays outlined.** "huge outlined 09, white" on an area
  box came out solid white with a drop shadow on one n15 try. An area box whose
  note says outlined / hollow / stroked is flagged `outlined`; codegen is told
  "transparent fill, 2px white text stroke, no shadow", and the page is checked:
  the biggest text over the box must have a text stroke and a see-through fill
  (the snapshot now records `-webkit-text-stroke-width`).
- **Text with no colour typed must read on what is behind it.** The nav links
  came out black on the red half. Every visible piece of text is checked against
  the topmost fill under its middle (a clipped half of a split page counts only
  inside its clip; a gradient counts as the mean of its stops; pictures are not
  judged). It fails when contrast is under 2, or when the text is near-black on a
  saturated fill where white reads at least as well (red, royal blue, purple).
  Text whose words appear in a note that names a colour keeps that colour.
  Codegen also gets the rule. Chrome's `oklab(...)` colours (text-white/70) now parse.

Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/textlook.test.ts` (12). Typecheck clean; full
suite 1535 pass, 2 fail as before (root-only permission tests, also red on the base).

black band came out wider than drawn, and try 1 set the headline narrower.

saw 7+ points between builds of one sketch), but the honest result is no gain.
