# n178: a pen press on a note's x draws; a tap in a tiny box fills that box

Found in n176 (round 43):
- **e2 RSVP:** two boxes were started where a just-typed note's x sat (the Maybe box on "Will you come?"'s x at
  557,312; its redraw on "Maybe"'s x at 723,360). Each box came back round to its start, so press and lift were
  both on the x: the note was deleted, the box was never drawn, and nothing said which note went. Two notes
  re-typed, one box drawn a third time.
- **e4 pet adoption:** an orange tap in the 18px Dogs checkbox was read as a page tap (marks under 20px could not
  hold a tap), so with the white page tap the page became an orange-to-white fade. Undone by hand.

## What was built
- **The x on a note draws in draw mode** (`overlay-client/src/main.ts` `wireNoteDrag`): a press on a chip's x that
  travels is a pen stroke from the press, like a press on the chip's words (n153). Only a press that stays put
  deletes, and the delete now says which note went (`note deleted: "Will you come?"`). Out of draw mode the x is a
  plain button as before.
- **Tiny closed marks hold taps** (`ink/src/inkfill.ts` `smallClosed`): a box or ring 10-20px that really goes
  round (ink about its perimeter, ends meeting) is a fill target, so a tap in a checkbox, radio or swatch fills
  it. A short tick or an open hook is still not a holder. Shared by the overlay (shown at once) and the build.
- Tests: `packages/ink/tests/n178-tiny-box-tap.test.ts` (4, on the real pet marks); `tests/e2e/chip-del-stroke.mjs`
  (zero tokens). Before the fix the e2e fails exactly like round 43 (`marks 1 -> 1`, note gone); after, it passes,
  and the n153 `chip-stroke.mjs` e2e still passes.
