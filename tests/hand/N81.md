# n81: words at the two ends of a long line keep their sides

Round 35 e4 (pharmacy) and e5 (split bill), n77/n80: the header was drawn as one rule across the page, the logo
typed over its left end and the links (or trip name) over its right end. A level line kept only its FIRST note
as its words, and a line spanning the page sits "centred" on it, so the build was told to centre the logo on
x 640: logo and links came out together in the middle (and the pharmacy header grew 75 to 108px). n77 needed a
fix note on both pages ("white top bar: halves far left, trip text far right").

## What was built (`lineends.ts`, zero tokens)
- A level line at least 600px long (or 60% of the page) with two or more notes typed at different parts of it
  (left third, middle, right third) keeps ALL of them, each with its side; it no longer takes the first note as
  its words, so it is never told to centre them and never sized as a heading by its length.
- Told: the left words left-aligned near the line's left end, the right words pushed to the right end
  (space-between / margin-left: auto), never one centred group; words in one group are separate items; a line
  near the top with its words above it is the bottom edge of a bar that tall.
- Checked on the page (`line-ends`): the left words start in the line's left quarter, the right words end in
  its right quarter; a miss goes to repair.
- A single note on a long line is left as before (a heading sized by its line, n56/n57).
