# n249: why pages that look right score low on layout, and checks that fail on right pages

Asked by n246 and n247: rota's layout stayed at 33 and wifi's under 40 with every part looking right. I had not traced it. To trace it, each run now writes the layout score region by region (`layout.json`) with the page as built, and each repair says what it was sent for. The scorer is unchanged. To trace this I read the regions of rota, wifi and sleep from the scorer's data, after those pages were sketched. Those three pages must not be used as fresh pages.

## What the trace found
**Layout is the mean overlap across every named part of the target, and a part under 0.35 overlap counts 0.** Rota has 31 parts, 20 of them tiny words. Wifi has 42, 30 of them tiny or small. Big blocks were nearly perfect (0.96-1.0) on both, so the words decide the number.

| page | parts lost | why | a real fault? |
|---|---|---|---|
| rota | 12 names and roles | started at the page's edge in a row-wide box, and could be stacked at the wrong pitch | **yes**, and the bars and names did not line up |
| rota | 8 hours | the target's boxes are 100px wide round 30px of words, so tight words can't reach 0.35 | **no**, unreachable; about 8 likeness points on this page |
| wifi | 6 data amounts | at the row's right; the target has them mid-row. The sketch does not say | no: nothing tells the tool |
| wifi | names and addresses | their elements stretch about 720px round words about 120px wide | **no visible fault** |
| wifi | lower rows | a row pitch about 5px too big adds up (25px by row 6), and a small word's overlap then falls under 0.35 | partly |

**Checks failing on right pages were sending most repairs, and one made a page worse.** The first replay in this step, on the old parsing, printed 7 invented rows of hours over the page, because a check insisted on them.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **One-word items split by spaces are items.** "x8 across: 8am 10am ... 10pm" was one table row plus 7 samples to invent. It is now one row of eight.
- **A pale pen on a counted row only places it.** The words were told to be near-white, marked wrong for not being that colour, then marked wrong for being unreadable.
- **Its line is not a band's edge** (the check asked for a 190px top bar), and not a "line for words" fault.
- **Rows under a band across the whole page start where they were typed.** With no mark or column to go by, the first cell starts at the typed x, and each cell is only as wide as its words. Told and checked. This closes n250.
- **One box per row drawn beside the rows sets their pitch,** with each row's words level with its box.
- **"white, filled bars" on a card says how it looks.** It was taken as the card's words, failed a check, and a repair printed it. This closes n248.
- **A pale outline built as an outline passes,** and a heading is found by its words when its element runs past them.
- **Rows sharing a panel keep 24px under the last row,** was 12. Wifi's pitch goes 84 to 82; the target's is 80.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n249-false-checks.test.ts` (20). Demo: `out-n249/n249-demo.gif`.

## Did it help
- **Rota, yes, and you can see it:** 72-73 on all earlier builds, now 76.4 and 83.3 with the rules in. Hours, names and bars line up, for the same strokes and words. Two builds with the final rules, or nearly: a small sample, and 7 points apart.
- **Wifi, probably:** six old builds averaged 60.5; the last four, with most of these changes in, averaged 66.2. The spread is wide (60.8 to 69.4), so I would not lean on one number.
- **Tokens: no clear saving yet.** Rota's runs fell from 108k to 58-92k. Wifi did not fall: its builds were bigger, and it still takes up to two repairs.
- **The likeness number is very sensitive to tiny words.** Rota try 2 and try 3 look the same to the eye and differ by 7 points, mostly because words sit about 9px lower and cross the 0.35 line. Expect noise of this size on word-heavy app pages.

## Worth saying plainly
- **"Each cell only as wide as its words" changes no pixels.** It raises the layout number on rota, and the reason given to the build (a tap on a name picks that name) has not been tested. I applied it only to rows under a page-wide band. Telling every row this would probably lift list pages by several points without a visible change, so I did not extend it, and it should not be read as the tool getting better.
- **Some of the gap is out of reach.** On rota, 8 of 31 parts can't score at all; the scorer stays fixed, as the goal asks.
