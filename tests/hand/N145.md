# n145: a list starts under its heading; a list lead is never copy; small rings stay round

Found in n139 / n144 (round 40 e2 recipe, e3 ride booking).

## What was built
- **A list note typed under its heading starts right under it** (`textplace.ts` `listUnderHeading`, used in `intent2.ts`):
  a list note (three or more items after a colon) typed in empty space 50 to 220px below a short note (four words or
  fewer, no colon) within 160px of its left, with no other note and no drawn mark between them, is told and checked at
  the heading's spot + 40px, not where it was typed. A person types a long note where there is room; the list belongs
  under its heading. Before, the recipe's ingredients started about 80px low.
- **A list lead is never copy, and spaced slashes split items** (`rowwords.ts` `listLead`, `listItems`): "steps: a / b; c
  / d" gives three items with no "steps:", and a semicolon inside one step stays in it. Semicolon lists lose a one to
  three word lead too, unless the items are label: value rows ("Mon: 9-5; Tue: 10-4").
- **A small ring stays round** (`shapemark.ts` `SMALL_RING_PX`): a closed stroke under 32px must fill its square to be a
  box; a rough 24px ring (map marker) touched every edge and corner and was built square.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 10 unit tests (`packages/pipeline/tests/n145-list-heading-ring.test.ts`).
