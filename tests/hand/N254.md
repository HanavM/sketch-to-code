# n254: why words stay over 50 a page, and what removes the biggest kind

Rounds 44-48 ran 54 to 75 words a page against a goal of 50. I counted every note on the 30 pages of rounds 46-48, from what was typed (no target files opened). `n254scan.mjs` reads what the tap rules change on all kept sketches, with no builds.

## Where the words go

| round | median words | first sketch only | fix notes | pages at 50 or under, first sketch only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 46 | 56 | 46.5 | 0 | 6 of 10 |
| 47 | 59.5 | 46.5 | 12.5 | 7 of 10 |
| 48 | 75 | 43.5 | 21 | 6 of 10 |

**The first sketch is already under 50 in every round. The fix notes put it over.** Fixes took 407 words of the 1,831 typed. So the way to fewer words is a first build that needs no fix, not shorter notes. That also fits the goal's rule against winning by wording.

The biggest single kind of fix note takes back something nobody drew: 72 of the 407 fix words are in notes like "flat one colour, no shade, no black edge" and "flat dark page, no fade". Next come parts built behind what was drawn over them, and rows or tiles laid out wrong, which earlier steps have been working through.

## What was built
- **A tap fills what was there when it was made.** A page tap stays the page's when a shape is drawn over the spot later. Puppet stage: the stage came out white and the beam a fade.
- **A tap on a mark's own line, in its own pen, is that mark's.** A hair outside the line it was read as a page tap, and the whole page became a fade to orange. That is the sleep tracker's fade, which survived every earlier step.
- **That tap on the line, with one other colour tapped well inside, only touched the line.** The line keeps its colour, the inside takes the other one, and there is no fade across the mark.
- **What is drawn later stands in front** where closed marks overlap. Told from the drawing order. A box lying inside a plain panel says nothing new and is left out.
- **Flat unless asked.** One colour per part: no second tone, gradient, shadow, dark edge or glow unless a note asks for one, or taps name a fade.
- Two taps well inside a banner, and two clear page taps, are still fades. Across 190 kept sketches the tap rules change 3 pages: these two and one more line tap (round 38 c2).
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n254-taps-stack-flat.test.ts` (17). Demo: `out-n254/n254-demo.gif`.

## Did it help
- **Yes on all three, and you can see it.** Six builds of six came out with the faults this step went after gone (page flat, stage dark, cars in front, mountains one colour), with no fix and fewer words, in about half the time of the old runs with fixes, on 30 to 50% fewer tokens.
- **Words:** 79 to 55, 91 to 43, 66 to 39, because the fix notes were no longer needed. If those three had gone this way in round 48, its median would have been about 51.5, not 75. That is a sum, not a measurement.

## Worth saying plainly
- **A replay leaves fix notes out by design,** so the word drops only hold if a person would not have typed them. Looking at the results, I would not on railway try 1 or puppet try 1. I would on puppet try 2, the highest score here: it printed "x8 vertical stripes, alternate darker" on both curtains in tiny words and underlined a heading line, and the scorer barely noticed. On sleep I might: the ring's arc goes all the way round, while the target has a thick ring with a dim quarter.
- **Spread is still wide:** railway 87.8 and 80.2 look much the same and differ mostly on layout.
- **Three pages picked because they had these faults.** A fresh round is the real test, and the next step writes one.
- **Flat unless asked has a cost I have not measured:** a page whose target has a soft shadow or fade now needs the sketcher to ask for it.
- One small change to the overlap rule (a big shape drawn over a small one) went in after these replays. It does not touch these three pages; unit tests only.
