# n244: a drawn box keeps its size when a count fills it

Found in n238 (round 48 e5, wifi). A panel drawn 560px tall, with a square and a pill in its first row, and "x6 down: ...". It built 390px tall, pulled the drawn button up 109px, and the pill got invented words (Online, Offline). "Taller rows" overshot off the screen. Scored 55.0 after two fixes, at 115 words and 260k tokens.

## Causes found
- **The tool told the build two opposite things.** The row template gave each row 65px, "no taller rows", from the drawn row alone. The mark check then said give the box its 562px. No repair could do both.
- **A pill was called a "picture or icon".** Nothing said it holds no words, so the build wrote some.
- **A tap on the first row's square reached only the first row.** Rows 2-6 got blank grey icons.
- **"x4 down" on the tall sidebar asked for four 896px-tall sidebars.** That false check failed on every run and sent repairs after it.
- **Found by the control replay, older than this step:** "5 rows like this, thin dividers: A Row A, B Row B, ..." was read as one row, so four rows were invented and the typed ones never printed.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Rows share a box with real room left:** evenly, when each row gets at least a quarter more height and stays within twice the drawn row. The box keeps its exact height and what is below it stays.
- **Any other box taller than its rows keeps its height too,** with the rows at the drawn pitch and the space under them left empty on purpose.
- **Sharing every box was checked on paper first and rejected.** Against the screenshots of the kept pages: wifi rows are about 80px (natural 64, shared 84), but transactions are 58 (natural 56, shared 66) and the night market 47 (natural 40, shared 70). Those two have empty space under their rows in the target, so a rule that always shares would have hurt them. Three pages set this threshold, so a fresh round has to confirm it.
- **A box never squeezes its rows:** one 116px box with "4 like this" is the first card, not a panel.
- **A pill at the row's end, under typed figures, is a toggle:** track and knob, no words, its tap the on colour. A typed "off" sets that toggle's state and is not printed. Under typed words ("Ana, Paid") it stays that word's badge.
- **A tap on a drawn part paints every row's copy,** unless a note names each row's colour.
- **A box half the page tall or more is never copied downward.**
- **Rows listed by commas after a look and a colon are the rows.** The rows check now accepts such a row split across its two columns (unit test only, not seen live).
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n244-rows-share.test.ts` (13). Demo: `out-n244/n244-demo.gif` (target, sketch, before, now).

## Did it help
- **Yes on effort, on both wifi builds:** 0 fixes against 2, 73 words against 115, 322-527 s against 814 s, 121-147k tokens against 260k.
- **What was asked for is right in 2 builds of 2:** panel height, button place, six rows, toggles, no invented words.
- **Likeness rose less, and varies:** 55.0 to 64.2 and 56.0. Two builds is a small sample. Wifi's layout score stays 34-39 and I did not trace all of it. The middle column went to the right end, and the stat words are still split (n246).
- **Control held, then improved:** night market 87.3 with a fix to 89.2 without one.

## Not done, or still off
- **Try 2's page ran past the screen,** the whole page showing about 15px up, which likely cost part of its 8 points against try 1. New node: n247.
- **"white, filled bars" was printed on the page in try 1.** Look words on a small card with no other copy. New node: n248.
- **"412 Mbps" stayed split or lost its Mbps in both tries.** That is n246.
- **All six icons came out orange,** as told. A person wanting different colours still needs a note.
- **The night market's repair chased the false rows check,** so its 148k tokens and 411 s include wasted repair. The check is fixed but was not re-run live.
