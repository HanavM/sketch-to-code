# n167: a ring drawn hollow stays hollow on every row; size words after a title are never printed

Found in n114 (round 42 e1 podcast: the hollow purple ring leading the episode rows was built as a filled disc on every
row; e4 dashboard: the title typed "Website relaunch big" printed "big").

## What was built
- **Small rings read as rings** (`shapemark.ts`): a closed stroke up to 48px across (was 32) must also fill its square
  to count as a box. The podcast's 36px ring, drawn as a 12-point circle, touched all four corners and was read as a
  small box, so the list mark was told "box filled purple" and built as purple squares (n167 try 1, thrown out).
- **A list's leading ring drawn with no tap is hollow** (`intent2.ts` `hollowRowMarks`): when a small ring leads a
  list (open-space rows or rows in a card), its pen fill is turned into a thin ring in the pen colour, and the rows
  brief says the mark is a HOLLOW ring on every row, never a solid disc (`rowMarkWords`). A tapped ring stays filled;
  a small square stays a square.
- **Size and weight words after a title** (`typedcopy.ts` `bareLookTail`): a trailing run of look words with no comma,
  holding at least one size or weight word, after two or more words of copy, is the look, not copy ("Website relaunch
  big" -> "Website relaunch" + big; "Slow Science huge bold"). "Think big", "Dream so big", "Overdue 3 red" stay
  copy. The page is checked that the note is not printed whole.
- 7 unit tests (`n167-hollow-row-ring.test.ts`). Zero tokens.
