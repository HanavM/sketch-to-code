# n90: sample rows put each value in its drawn column; stat cards read whole; email rows the short way

n89 left three gaps: on transactions the sample rows put the amount in the drawn middle column and a date at the
drawn right end; the stat cards put the number above the label though the note said "small grey label, big number
under"; and email compose was not re-sketched with its rows given the short way.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- `rowtemplate.ts` `sampleRoles`: when nothing is typed for the rows, the brief says which kind of value goes in
  which drawn column: column 1 the name, a middle column a secondary detail (category and/or date, or a subject),
  and a right-aligned end column the row's figure (amount, price, count or time). People read a list's right edge
  as its figure column; the drawn middle line had no words to say otherwise.
- `statcard.ts`:
  - `statOrder`: a look note saying where the label goes ("small grey label, big number under", "label on top",
    "number below") sets the order for every stat card; when the action's own notes don't say it, the page's notes
    do (the look note had bound to only 2 of the 4 cards).
  - Found in the try 1 prompt: "Income 3,800" was read as value "3" (split at the thousands comma), "Biggest day
    Oct 3" as value "3" with label "Biggest day Oct", and "Budget left 18%" lost "left" (a look word). Now a comma
    inside a number is no break, a day after a month name is a date value, and left/right are look words only
    next to "aligned".
- The ~20px page drift from n89 did not come back on either page (the drift check passed: 7 of 7 blocks within
  16px on transactions); no change made for it.
