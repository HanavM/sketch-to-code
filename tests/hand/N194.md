# n194: no tap = an outline in the pen colour, tap = a fill (any pen, any surface)

Found sketching round 44 (n187): c3 retro cinema's three show-time pills were drawn in a yellow pen on the dark
page and only the middle one tapped; the two untapped ones were built filled yellow, and the fix note turned them
into bare text with no border. c5 pottery's dark-pen "Book a seat, bold, dark outline" button came out a filled
dark pill. Earlier rules only outlined dark pens on light pages (n144) and light or grey pens (n78, n131, n141).

## What was built
- **A box whose own note says outline** (outline, outlined, outline only, border only, ghost, hollow; not "outlined
  letters"), and asks no fill, is an outline in its pen colour, any pen, any surface.
- **A box left untapped beside a tapped look-alike in the same pen** (about the same size, not a redraw on the same
  spot) is an outline: the picked one of a row of pills is filled, the others are rings of the pen colour.
- Told to the build as a 2px pen-colour border, see-through inside, never a bare label, pill ends kept round,
  words readable on the surface; checked on the page (border there, no pen-colour fill), with a repair line if not.
- Not caught: lanterns drawn in the pen of a tapped button (different size, stay filled glows), a frame drawn twice
  over its tapped self (round 38 film fest).

Wider check, the rule over the first hand sketch of every page so far (243 pages): 7 boxes change, all to outlines,
all outlines in their screenshots (cinema 2 pills, pottery button, yoga "See timetable", lighthouse "Check dates",
arcade "INSERT COIN", shape museum "Book tickets"). Each of the last four had typed "outline" in its note.
