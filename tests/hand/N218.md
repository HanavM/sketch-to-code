# n218: short heading lines land on their drawn lines; a day and a time stay one phrase

Found in n164 (round 41 c4, chess night). "CHECK / MATE huge bold caps, MATE outlined only" went on two drawn lines. One build
set CHECK at half its line with MATE far under it; the next put MATE on the date line. "Every Thursday, 7pm" came out as two
pieces in three builds in a row.

## Causes found
- **The heading had no placement at all.** Read live, a note typed "on these 2 lines" gives neither line its own words, and lines
  133px apart form no stack, so no drawn position or size reached the build, and nothing checked it. (My first version keyed on the
  stack and never fired; the two "comma fix only" rows below are from that version.)
- **The date was the list-row rule.** N164 said its cause was not the pair rule; it was a different one, `labelValue` from n195: "Every
  Thursday, 7pm, bold" reads as label + value, built at the two ends of the line.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Heading lines told one by one.** Lines with one note saying which words go where (a slash, or one word per line): each piece on its
  own line, letters' bottom on it, one size so the longest runs its drawn length. Checked on the page piece by piece; a miss goes to repair.
- **A day and a time on a line of their own are one phrase.** Ruled in a list ("Monday, 9am") it stays a row.

## Did it help
- **The two things asked for are fixed in both real builds**: both heading lines on their drawn lines at the drawn size, nothing touching the
  date, and the date in one piece. Structure rose from 86 to 92.
- **The likeness average did not clearly rise**: 85.7 and 74.9 against 79.3 and 83.3. The low one is a different fault (below). Sample of two; both
  pages have been seen, so neither counts toward done.

## Still off, new leads
- A drawn pattern's cream squares came out as grey fades in try 2 (layout 59). n164 saw a milder version. Nothing checks a pattern's named colours.
- "MATE outlined only" was dropped in one build of two, and the direction check passes anyway: it only keeps words off the page.
- Lines 133px apart form no stack in the reader. Told through the new rule here; left as is for other stacks.
