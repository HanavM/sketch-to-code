# n88: heading words that overflow their line wrap onto the stack drawn under it; tall-header drift re-checked

Same strokes, taps and notes as n86 (before its fix notes), replayed word for word (replay.mjs). First build only, no fixes.

## Gaps found (from n86)
- **A heading of 2-3 drawn lines bound its note to the first line only** (round 36 c4 florist): three level lines
  ~62px apart (a ~57px heading) sit just under the 64px pitch at which stacked lines read as one heading, so
  "Fresh flowers, at your door by noon. huge bold" was "on this line" and the build made one small cut line,
  centred. Its third line was also taken for a list's fourth row because the paragraph's wrap line under it sat
  on the same pitch.
- **The page drifted down from a tall header** (c5 tide swim, layout 20 in n86): re-measured with the tool as it is
  now (n81 header ends, n92) before changing anything: the first build put the nav on its line and every block
  where drawn (drift check: 4 of 5 blocks within 16px). Tide went **60.9 to 78.1** with no change in this step, so
  that half of n88 was already closed by n81's header rules. The "fix that lifts content deleted the header" case
  was a fix-note path; not re-run here (no fixes in these replays).

## What was built (zero tokens)
- `textmark.ts` (lineStacks): left-aligned level lines 48-63px apart are one heading when the words typed on the
  first line cannot fit on it (about half an em per character at the pitch's font size, with 25% slack), each line
  under it wraps no longer than the one above, and those lines are bare (no note of their own). Such a stack is
  sized as heading type (pitch / 1.1), not as a pull quote.
- `textmark.ts`: a line held tight by a paragraph's first line (a wrap) is never the "fourth row" that turns a
  three-line heading into a list.
- `notefocus.ts` `overflowStack` (used by noteFocus and by the overlay when a note is committed): a note whose words
  overflow the stack lines it landed on covers the whole stack (heading type, 28px and up). A column of short links,
  each with its own note, keeps one line per note.
- Tests: `packages/ink/tests/n88-heading-copy.test.ts` (4).
