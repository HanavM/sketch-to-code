## Round 47 whole (c1-c5 from N221, e1-e5 here)

Mean likeness **81.6** (c 76.8, e 86.4), median **18** strokes, median **59.5** words, median **8.9** min,
12 fixes across 10 pages, 1.49M billed tokens. Over the 80 line and inside the stroke limit, but 9.5 words over the
50-word limit, so this round does not count toward done. Words are now the blocker: on the app pages the first
build took a median of 47 words; fix notes pushed them over. Six of ten pages are harder than round 1.

**Be careful with e5's 90.3.** Its second list is wrong: three cards hold invented rows (Subscriptions $85,
Utilities $140, Savings $300) and the real rows stay squeezed into the first card. The scorer does not read words,
so this does not show in the score. A person would not call this page finished.

## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A ring with four taps of colour became a square gradient** (e5): the overlay preview already showed a fade and
  the first build painted a square of it. A fix note ("real round donut: flat slices, no fade, cream hole") then
  built a true donut, exactly right. Lead: a ring with several colour taps inside is a slice chart, first build.
- **"x4 down" on one drawn card squeezed all four rows inside it** (e5, the same family as n223): a fix asking for 4
  separate cards did nothing. I drew the three missing cards: the tool filled them with invented rows, and a
  further fix ("only Rent here; Food, Fun, Transport replace the three below") ran 224 s and changed nothing, with
  no message saying so. Lead: a count down repeats the box; listed rows go into drawn boxes in order; never invent a
  row beside listed ones; a run that changes nothing says so.
- **A box drawn around a tapped box became a solid fill on top** (e4): a yellow outline just outside the fourth tile
  became a yellow tile, hiding its colour and circle. Fix worked, though its brown came out darker than drawn. Lead:
  an untapped box hugging a filled box is its border.
- **"Name, value" notes stay in one piece** (e3, e5): "Lisbon, 52%" printed with the comma and no gap; "On track to
  save this month, $600" was centred with a comma left in after the fix. The same form on a row with a pen line was
  split right (e1). 19 of e3's 27 fix words went on this and the outline button. Lead: on a full-width bar or a label
  above a bar, a comma pair goes to the two ends, never printing the comma.
- **Row words took the pen colour of the line under them** (e1): light grey lines gave near-invisible row text, and
  the lines themselves were dropped. A fix put them back, 24px below their drawn place. Layout 58 despite a page that
  looks right.
- **A big number filled its bar** (e2): "Total hours, 40 big" made a 40 taller than the bar, in the middle, clipped.
  One short fix was enough.
- **An untapped outline next to a tapped button came out filled** (e3): "Share" drawn in the same pen, no tap. My fix
  said "both buttons full pills" and restyled both: a fix naming "both" carries the outline to both, needing a third
  round. (Same family as n224.)
- **Dragging a free note onto a box said "now whole sketch"** (e5): the note did not bind even dropped on the card's
  edge; deleting and retyping it on the card worked. Twice lost effort. The toolbar also moved itself to mid-page.

  3 drawn stalls.
- Timesheet: five typed names, "hours, totals bold", made the whole table with a cream total column from two lines
  and three boxes; numbers were made up as asked by leaving them out.

- One short note made a real donut chart with exact slice shares (e5).
- Video call: four tiles, one circle copied to all, four controls: 92, the best page of the round.
