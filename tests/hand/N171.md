# n171: a note typed just above a drawn button is its own line, never put inside the button

Found in n166 (round 42 e3, cart): "Total $199 bold", typed 37px above the black box drawn for Checkout (which had
"Checkout" typed inside it), was bound to the rule, the button and the panel at once; the build put the total
inside the button both tries and the word Checkout was lost (try 1 scored 42.5).

## What was built
- **A labelled box keeps its words** (`ink/notefocus.ts` `labelledBoxes`, shared by the overlay and the pipeline):
  inside a drawn container, a closed box that holds its own note (a button with its label typed in it) leaves the
  focus of a note typed outside it. The note goes to the container's open space at the spot it was typed. A box with
  no label of its own still takes a note typed next to it.
- **Told and checked** (`pipeline/textplace.ts` `boxUnder`): words typed in a big box just above a smaller box inside
  it (its top 4 to 70px below the spot) are told to the build as their own line just above that box, never inside it;
  the page is checked that none of the note's copy words (capitalised or with a digit) are built inside that box.
- 4 unit tests (`n171-above-button.test.ts`, the real n162 cart strokes and notes). Zero tokens.
