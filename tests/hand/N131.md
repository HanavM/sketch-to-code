# n131: a light coloured-pen box with no tap on a light surface is an outline

Found in n126/n127 (round 39): a person reaches for a light pen to draw the faint frame they see and taps when
they want a fill, but only neutral light greys (n78) were read that way. e1 flight search: the three slate-grey
inputs (#cbd5e1, two units too blue for n78) came out grey blocks; e4 vet booking: the mint "Luna" pill and the mint
slot area came out mint panels; e3 focus timer: the pink "+ Add task, dashed" box came out a pink block.

## What was built
- **Light pen outline** (`inkcolour.ts` `inkLightOutline`, used in `intent2.ts`): a box in a light pen of any hue
  (luminance 180-236), at least 16x12, with no tap, on a light surface, is an outlined block in the pen colour
  (dashed when a note says dashed), never a fill or tint. A note on the box asking for a fill keeps the fill. Tapped
  boxes are untouched (the timer's tapped pink tabs and Reset button stay filled).
- **The surface behind** (`surfaceBehind`): the smallest closed mark holding the box that is tapped or drawn in a
  filling pen, looking through light-pen holders (they are outlines themselves), else the page's tapped colour, else
  white. A light pen card on a dark page stays a fill.
- Told in the brief and checked on the page with n78's frame check (a border there, no fill in the pen colour).
- Zero tokens, no model call. 4 unit tests.
