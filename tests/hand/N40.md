# n40: big heading lines drawn far apart are one heading, at the drawn spot

Round 32 c1 (aurora): NORTH and LIGHTS were drawn as two level lines 170px apart, the date line 90px under
LIGHTS. The overlay groups a note's marks by distance (within 120px), so the heading note took line 1 only,
and LIGHTS' line joined the date line: the date note was "on these 2 marks", its thread drew one box round
LIGHTS and the date. The build also put the letters about half a line low (NORTH's baseline 56px under
its drawn line): a line was read as the middle of its letters, but this heading was drawn on its baselines.

## What was built
- **A giant heading's lines are one drawing** (packages/ink/src/notefocus.ts `giantStacks`, `stackJoin`, used
  by the overlay's note grouping): the lines of a stack read as one display heading (letters 60px+) always
  group together, however far apart, and a heading line never groups with another text line outside it.
  Shown on the sketch before the build: "NORTH LIGHTS huge white" is now "on these 2 lines" with one box
  round both; the date note is "on this line".
- **Heading lines are drawn low on the letters** (packages/ink/src/textmark.ts): of six hand-drawn heading
  stacks (rounds 1, 31, 32) four were drawn on the baseline and two through the middle. The heading's box
  now starts 0.16 of a line higher than centred, so letters end just at or a little under the drawn lines
  (a quarter of a capital off at most either way); the build brief says to fill the box from its top.
- Unit tests: the aurora strokes as a fixture (before: line 1 alone, LIGHTS with the date; after: both lines
  together, date alone); a box drawn round a heading still groups with it.
