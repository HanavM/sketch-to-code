# n124: a bar its own row shows as zero is empty on purpose, not a collapse to repair

Found in n123 (round 38 e3, budget): the "Fun $0 of $300" bar was built at 0% width, the collapsed-fill check
flagged it "renders with 0 width", and both runs spent two repair rounds (about 5 minutes, ~100k tokens) on a bar
that was right.

## What was built
- **Zero rows excuse an empty fill** (`collapsed.ts`): before a 0-width or 0-height fill is reported, the tool reads
  the words of that bar's own row (the texts under its nearest ancestor that holds 8 text nodes or fewer and no other
  bar from the same line of code; failing that, the text line directly above the bar's track and any on its own
  line, within 48px). When that row shows a zero amount ("$0 of $300", "0%", "0 / 5", "0 of 12 done", "€0.00"), the
  fill is left alone and the run's stages say `not flagged: ... its row reads "..."`. A zero in a neighbouring row
  never excuses a bar; "$0.50", "10 / 5" and "100%" are not zero.
- Zero tokens, no model call.
