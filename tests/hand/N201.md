# n201: a count on one drawn box repeats it beside it; characters for drawn boxes go inside them

Found in n198 (round 45): two app pages lost points and needed a fix because a count typed on a drawn mark was
read the wrong way.

## Why it happened
- **Audio mixer (e3):** one channel strip drawn (a tall box with a ring, a fader line and a knob box inside), and
  on it "6 strips: Drums, Bass, Keys, Guitar, Vocals, FX; knobs at different heights". The build kept the drawn
  strip as Drums and crammed five tiny strips into its foot, leaving the open space beside it empty. Nothing told
  it that one drawn thing plus a count means "that many of this, side by side, where there is room".
- **Two-factor code (e2):** six small boxes drawn in a row, "4, 8, 1 big bold in first three boxes" typed in the
  first. The build made three huge bare digits and dropped the boxes they were for; "big bold" was taken as the
  look of the whole thing, not of the digit inside its box.

## What was built (`repeatmark.ts`, zero tokens)
- **A count on one drawn box repeats that box at its drawn size** ("6 strips: ...", "4 cards like this", "x5"):
  the drawn box is copy 1 of N; the copies go side by side into the open space to its right, up to the next drawn
  mark or the page edge (or stacked below when a wide box has no room sideways), at the drawn gap, each with the
  drawn insides at the same offsets and the k-th typed name. Never copies inside the drawn box.
  Rows, lines, bars, dots, stars, tabs and plain counts ("3 colours") are left to the rules that already read them.
- **Characters typed for a row of drawn boxes go inside them** ("4, 8, 1 in first three boxes"): the k-th
  character centred in the k-th box, every drawn box kept at its drawn size, size words are the character's look.
- Both are told to the build and **checked on the page** (N look-alike boxes of about the drawn size along the
  row; each character inside a box that still shows as a box). A failed check goes to the repair round.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n201-repeat-mark.test.ts` (8).
- Wide check, 450 hand-sketch digests (every page sketched so far): the new rules fire on exactly these two
  notes and nowhere else, so no other page changes.

## Did it help
- **Mixer: yes, a lot.** 76.8 → 90.1 and 89.9 with no fix at all, 22 fewer words, about 6-8 minutes less from
  first stroke to done, half the tokens.
- **Two-factor: effort yes, likeness mixed.** The boxes now stay and hold the digits on the first build (no fix,
  11 fewer words, 2 minutes less), but 86.5 / 88.6 is below the 92.0 the fixed page reached: "big bold" still
  makes the digits larger than the target's, and the box borders come out faint. Next lead: a size word on a
  character inside a small drawn box means "as big as fits comfortably", not huge.
- Still open on the mixer: the drawn knob rings are built as filled discs (untapped ring outline gap, n199).
