## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A ring drawn in a coloured pen is built as a solid disc** (e2 thermostat, e3 timer, 2 of 2): "orange arc
  over grey track" / "red arc 3/4 on pink track" both came out as a filled circle; a 2-word "hollow ring" fix
  made each right. Lead: a ring noted as an arc, track or progress is a hollow ring of the drawn thickness.
- **Pen-colour boxes are filled with the pen colour** (e1 grey inputs, e4 mint slot area, e3 pink dashed
  box): a light-grey outline meant "input border", not a grey fill. Lead: a light pen box with no tap
  inside a white area is an outline (border colour), never a fill.
- **Dark-pen boxes on a dark page built white** (e2): three cards drawn in dark pen came out white; the fix
  "right cards dark" reached only one of the three (plural fix note binds one element).
- **A group note binds to the one box under it** (e4 day row): "Mon 14, Tue 15, Wed 16..." on the Wed box
  gave Wed "14 Mon"; the fix "Wed 16" replaced one span's text literally (now "14 / Wed 16").
- **Words meant as directions printed on the page** (e4): "selected, avatar" inside the pill, "12 time
  slots 09:00-16:30, 4 a row" as a caption over the grid.
- **A short line under a heading is pasted as an underline** (e2 "Living room", e3 "Today", e4 "Book a
  visit"): the line meant "text goes here".
- **Made-up data where a list is described, not typed** (e4 slots 12:00/13:00 instead of 14:00/14:30,
  e5 six invented expense rows): expected, but costs structure.
- **A drawn line at a table's top rule** (e5) went above the header, not under it.
