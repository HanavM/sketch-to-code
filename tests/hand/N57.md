# n57: do the gains carry to the agency and ticket pages? Outlined words on a line, and "same"

Replayed the n49 sketches of round 33 c2 (slow agency, 52.5 after 2 fixes) and c3 (gig ticket, 78.1 after 1 fix)
word for word with everything through n56: first build only, no fix notes, no note drags.

## What the replay found (gaps)
- **Agency: "SLOW, giant outlined letters" on a line came out solid; "WORK, same" came out solid and small.**
  Hollow letters were only told and checked for a box round a big word (n16), never for words typed on a line, and
  "same" was read as nothing. Try 1 (old code) even split SLOW into "SL ... W" around the red circle. 45.9.
- **One giant word on a line was never sized or checked**: the line-span check (n45/n56) skipped a single word,
  counted "outlined" and "letters" as copy, and skipped any line in a stack (SLOW over WORK), while the stack check
  only judges text of 3+ words. So nothing held SLOW at the drawn 866px.
- **"all text white" fought the other notes**: its check asked the grey tagline ("light grey" typed) and the outlined
  letters to go white, and its repair filled SLOW solid white (tries 4-5).
- **Ticket: the first build now keeps the tilted ticket** (n49 dropped it; 60.8, 63.7 with no fixes vs 78.1 after a
  fix note), but the white tap and the pink tap either side of the drawn divider were read as one white-to-pink fade
  over the whole ticket, the drawn text lines were left as visible rules, and the heading came out one small line.

## What was built
- **Words typed on a line can be outlined** (`textplace.ts` `linkSame`, `textlook.ts`): "outlined / hollow / stroked"
  in a line's note flags it; the build is told hollow letters (transparent fill, text stroke) kept as one word, and
  the page is checked: the text carrying those words must be stroked and see-through.
- **"same" copies the look of the line above** (`resolveSame`): "WORK, same" takes the style part of the nearest
  noted line above (within 320px), also across actions (the line above is usually its own action).
- **A giant word or two on a long line is sized by the line** (`linespan.ts` `isDisplay`): told the font-size that
  spans the line and checked, even inside a stack; one short word may go up to 300px. Style words like "outlined",
  "letters", "same" are no longer taken as copy.
- **"all text white" leaves words given their own colour and hollow letters alone** (`alltext.ts` `ownColourText`).
