# n54: a big box round a page of content is a card, and wide boxes keep their drawn sides

Round 33 e3 (invoice): the big box drawn round the whole invoice was built as nothing (all grey page), and
"Total £1,820, big bold", typed on a line inside it, made the box a big-word area, so a giant "1042" was
built over the page. Two fix notes were needed (80.6 after three builds). Round 33 e5 (billing): two panels
drawn 200-1080 were built 216-1063, centred so inside the 28px slack, costing about 14 points of layout.

## What was built
- **A box that holds 3+ other drawn marks is a container, never a big-word area** (`areas.ts` `holdsMarks`,
  `markAreasAndEdges`): its big/bold notes belong to the lines inside it. Counted over every stroke on the page,
  since the box is often its own action and its lines sit in others.
- **That container (240x120 or more, not named a picture, field, map or "no card") is a card** (`markCards`,
  `cardsBrief`): white (or its tapped colour), light border, at exactly the drawn left/top/width/height,
  everything drawn inside it inside it. Checked on the page (`verifyCards`): a panel must show there, each edge
  within 8px of the drawn edge, or the repair is told which edges and the exact numbers.
- **Each side of a box 300px or wider lands within 8px of its drawn side** (`shapemark.ts`, `BOX_EDGE_PX`):
  a centred panel built narrower than drawn is named with both spans.
