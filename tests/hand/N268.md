# n268: a note about looks is never printed, and words typed under a box stay under it

Found by the n266 redraw of the library search page (r46 e1), which fell from 80.3 to 64.4 with two fixes and 94 words.

## What actually went wrong
The tool's own checks did the damage. "four covers solid" was taken as the first cover's words, so one check demanded it on
the page and a repair printed it over the title; a second check then pulled the words to where the note was typed. Words typed
under a cover were told "beside or inside" their box, so the first build put them inside.

## What was built
- **A note about how a kind of thing looks prints nothing:** a count or "all", a plural, then look words ("four covers solid",
  "pale chips solid", "tracks thick black"), or one thing plus a fill word ("button hollow"). Told to the build, and checked.
  Not a box's words, not placed words. Across 1,982 kept notes it catches 7, all really looks (`n268scan.mjs`).
- **A capitalised name made of colour words is copy:** "The Deep Blue bold" had no words left once look words went, so the first cover had no title of its own.
- **Words typed under a box of real size stay under it:** in order, below its bottom edge, in its column; the box keeps its drawn size and is not a card round them. Told and checked. Boxes under 60 px are left out, since a note under a pill may just be where it landed.
- **A colour built as a border counts:** the pale header rule built right as a bottom border failed its colour check on every run, costing a repair of about 40k tokens on an otherwise clean page.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n268-under-and-look-notes.test.ts` (12).
