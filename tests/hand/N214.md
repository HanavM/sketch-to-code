bold right". "x4" was read as "this row and three sample rows": "down: Loan amount $24,000 half" was printed as a label,

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A count with that many items after it is those items** (`drawnlist.ts`, `countedItems`): "x4 down:", "4 sliders:",
  then exactly N items split by ';' (or ', '). A lower-case part says how every row looks. An item is its label, its
  value and, when a value is there, how far along it is ("half", "70%"): the share, count and lead are never printed.
- **Rows go on the drawn lines** (`drawnLists`): the note on or just above a drawn line takes that line and the like
  lines below, at their drawn pitch. A shorter line over the first and a small ring at its end are every row's filled
  part and knob, ending at that row's own share. One line with a filled part drawn repeats down. A lone plain line is left
  to the row template (it may be a word's line in a row).
- **No sample rows** (`rowData`, `likeCount`): such a note now gives its typed rows and asks for none invented.
- **Rival tells removed**: the row template read the same note as "rows 44px tall" and its check squeezed the rows
  on a repair (early replay below); typed copy told "x4 down: Loan amount $24,000 half" as text. Both now step aside.
- **Checked on the page** (`verifyDrawnLists`): each label and value at its line, tracks and filled parts at their
  spans, no other words in the rows' band, no count words printed.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n214-drawn-list.test.ts` (9). Demo: `out-n214/n214-demo.gif` (target, before, after).
