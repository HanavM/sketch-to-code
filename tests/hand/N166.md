# n166: the toolbar steps off the drawing; rows typed beside a column of pictures run up to the panel

Found in n162 (round 42): on e1 podcast a white colour tap for the play button landed on the bar's "manual" button
(the bar sat over the drawn player band) and switched the overlay into test mode, losing every stroke after it; the
sketcher had to drag the bar away by hand. On e3 cart the rows, typed in open space beside three drawn thumbnails,
ran only to x 660 (price at 636), where the drawn space and the target run to about 760, just short of the summary panel.

## What was built
- **The bar never sits on the drawing** (`overlay-client` `stepBarOffInk`): when a finished stroke runs under the bar,
  the bar moves to the nearest free spot (bottom, top, corners, sides) with no ink under it and says so. Its buttons
  stay buttons; a later tap there lands on the ink because the bar is gone.
- **Rows beside a column of like boxes** (`openrows.ts` `columnLed`): a rows note in open space, with 2+ like boxes
  stacked down its left (one picture per row) and no field above, starts level with the first box, rows a box apart,
  and runs from the column's left to just short of the panel drawn on its right (the column's own left margin, at
  most 60px), or to the same margin from the page edge. The row's last value (a price) sits at its right end. Told
  to the build and checked on the page (first row's start, row width).
- **One row per `;`**: a rows note given whole rows separated by `;` (each with its own commas) reads one row per
  segment; a lower-case segment (`dividers`) is a look. The first row's name is what is checked.
- 5 unit tests (`n166-column-rows.test.ts`, the real cart strokes). Zero tokens.
