## What was built
- **Ink overrules "all text white" where the pen coloured text** (`alltext.ts` `inkTextOf`, `onInkText`): text
  lines drawn in a colour far from the all-text colour are named in the brief as exceptions, with their spot and hex,
  and the all-text check leaves text on those lines alone (the ink colour check still holds them to their colour).
- **A repair that sized a heading to its drawn line is kept** (`linespan.ts` `spanMisses`, `keepSpanRepair`; run2
  `putBackIfWorse`): when the repair took line-span misses down, it is not put back for moving boxes, unless they moved
  by more than 160px in all.
- **Two words typed on their own drawn lines that touch are put back on their lines** (`ownwords.ts` `lineOf`): the
  overlap repair names each word's drawn line and says to keep each baseline there, tighten line-height, shrink only
  if still touching, and never move one below its line.
