# n11: a stacked headline is one heading (goal 2026-10-27)

What was built (branch try/n4-look-fix, on top of n10's try/n3-ink-style):

- **Stacked level lines are one heading.** Two or three level straight lines,
  same left edge or centred, at a big even pitch (64-200px), each no longer than
  the one above and none page-wide, read as one display heading
  (`lineStacks` in `@s2c/ink`). Before, only squiggles could stack: a long line
  with a short centred line under it was "a heading and its smaller line".
  Four or more even lines stay a ruled list; body lines (tighter pitch) and a
  zigzag are never a heading line.
- **A note on any line covers all of them**, shown at once ("on these 2 lines").
- **Pitch sets the size, the drawing sets the breaks.** The stack gives codegen
  fontPx from the pitch and each line's drawn length (`lineWidths`): break the
  copy where the drawing breaks, regular weight unless a note says bold, never
  more lines than drawn. The action's region takes the stack's line boxes, so
  the letters are centred on the drawn lines.
- **A note typed in a small box inside a big one is about the small box only**
  (a button on a card, a badge on a panel).

Tests: `packages/ink/tests/linestack.test.ts`, three new note-focus cases; one
old expectation updated (round 4 c2's "Shop" button note no longer reaches the
dark half it sits in).

- Sunset, try 1: the heading came out at the drawn size and place, but bold and
  wrapped "Stay for the / golden hour" (wider letters), so it lost points.
  Try 2 (line breaks from the drawing): "Stay for the golden / hour", regular

  (structure 0.92 -> 0.95). Layout fell (0.56 -> 0.46: nav and subtitle sit a

- Diagonal: all 8 notes landed on the right mark first time (no drags needed,

  vertical with a separate band: 55.1 -> 39.8. Neither is the heading; both
  are older gaps (a box round a big word becomes a card; the band not read as

## Gaps found this step
- A run's repair round can stall with nothing written: no result, no times,
  no tokens (sunset try 2). The verify flagged "dusk." because a line drawn for
  a word is 1px tall and the built word is 32px tall at the same spot.
- A box round a big word or number still sometimes becomes a visible card
  (diagonal: a black card behind the 09) despite the n7 rule.
- A diagonal band plus "lime left, red right" can come out as a vertical split
  with the band drawn on top, not the band as the edge between the colours.
- Scores swing a lot between builds of the same sketch (sunset 67 vs 74 with a
  small prompt change); one build per page is a noisy before/after.
