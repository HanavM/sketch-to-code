  as a link (an arrow, see / view / read / learn / explore ...) is told as the link's place AND its underline in
  that ink, left out of the no-rules check, and checked: an underline or bottom border under the words (or a thin bar
  just under them), and on ONE line. Snapshot now records underlined text.
- **A sentence on one drawn line runs its length**: 4+ copy words on one level line (not a heading, not a stack)
  whose length implies body type (13-26px) is told its size and checked (90-120% of the line, one line).
- **Capital words touch by their letters** (`ownwords.ts` `inkBand`): text in capitals and digits only is judged by
  its cap band (about 0.74em on the baseline), not its whole line box, in the words-on-words check.
