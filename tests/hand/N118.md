# n118: a ring drawn in coloured arcs is that chart

Found in n108/n116 (round 38 e3, budget): the spending donut was drawn as four arcs chained into one ring, each in its
own pen colour (orange from the top round to the lower right, teal along the bottom, indigo up the left, light grey back
to the top). The build made a donut of its own: its own size and place, and its own segment order (grey at the top,
orange starting at one o'clock). The drawn arcs, which say where each colour starts and how much of the ring it takes,
were thrown away.

## Built
- **Drawn charts** (`drawnring.ts`): from the strokes alone, 2 to 8 coloured arcs, each starting where the last ended
  (within 30px), closing on themselves, going round at least 300 degrees and about round, are one chart. Each arc's
  colour, start angle and share of the turn are measured (budget: orange 39%, teal 26.5%, indigo 19%, grey 15%, start at
  12 o'clock; the target's amounts are 40 / 25 / 19 / 16). The build is told the chart's drawn centre and size, that it is
  a perfect circle (not a shape traced from the strokes), and the segments in order, using the notes' amounts when given.
  Checked on the page: a round element within 20% of the drawn size and 25px of the drawn centre. Zero tokens.
- Try 1 built the right segments inside a clipped octagon (the drawn outline used as a clip); the brief now says a
  perfect circle, never a clip traced from the strokes. Try 2 was round.
