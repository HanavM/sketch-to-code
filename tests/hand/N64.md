# n64: giant capital words sit ON their drawn lines

Replayed the n49 sketch of round 33 c2 (slow agency: 9 strokes, 34 words, 7 notes) word for word: first build only,
no fix notes, no note drags.

## Gap (from n62)
SLOW and WORK were drawn as two long lines at the letters' bottoms (y 382 and 636). Every n62 build set them at full
width but about 40px above their lines: the brief said "letters sitting on y", the model read that as the text box,
and nothing checked it (the span check matched words only within 110px of the line by their box middle, which a
290px word misses).

## What was built
- **Told:** a giant word or two on a long line is told the line is its letters' BASELINE (bottom edge on the line,
  block top about 0.85 of the font-size above it with line-height 1).
- **Checked** (`linespan.ts`, `baselineOf` in `ownwords.ts`): the one-line heading check now finds display words by a
  window that grows with their size, and fails when their baseline is more than max(16px, 7% of the size) off the
  line, naming how far to move just that word (relative top / translateY, size kept).
