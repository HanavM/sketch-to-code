## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A tap on a box sitting on a ring's line fills the ring** (c1): the corner cabin's tap, in the ring's own pen,
  landed on the ring's line, and the whole wheel came out a solid yellow disc hiding the spokes. Likely cause, not yet
  traced: the n254 rule (a tap on a mark's own line in its own pen is that mark's) catching a tap meant for the small box.
- **A rough dome with a flat base has no way to be a half circle** (c2): nine waypoints built nine flat facets. A fix
  asking for "smooth half circles" built a rounded box with straight sides. The page scored layout 33 after that fix;
  the first build was not scored, so I cannot say the fix lowered it. Rings get snapped to circles; half circles do not.
- **A hook line built as a filled shape** (c2): a J line for a handle came out a thick blob, and the fix made it thin
  and doubled it on two of three handles.
- **A word typed beside a line went onto it** (c2): "$48" typed 30 px right of the handle was bound to it and printed
  on it, white. The bar said so ("on this scribble"), so a person could have seen it first, but moved nothing.
- **Colours tapped along a count made a fade under it** (c3): one box "x5 huts" with five taps gave five right huts,
  but the sand box those taps sat in became a rainbow fade (likely the taps, not yet traced). Fix: 10 words.
- **Row spacing ignored the drawn ends** (c3): first and last hut were drawn, but the middle three came out unevenly
  spaced until a fix asked.
- **Three notes bound to a box were built nowhere** (c3): "Sleeps four", "Kettle and deckchairs", "From $180 a week"
  were lost and a first fix did not bring them; I had to type them again. Nothing said they were missing.
- **A line inside a tapped box cut its fill** (c4): the label's rule split the label, cream above and dark below with
  a white border. A note had to say "whole label cream".
- **Rounded corners lost on a big drawn box, and a drawn pill built an oval** (c4): cassette square, window an oval.
- **Dark holes in the reels ask for a word and got a small dot** (c4, also c5 hub): a ring within a ring has no quick
  way to be drawn.
- **An untapped scoop stayed an outline** (c5): the pale circle on the cone was built as a ring, which I left as is.
- **Cost of strokes** (c4): 24, over the limit, because four tiles take a box and a tap each. Ideas for fewer: one box
  plus taps along a count, if the fade above is fixed.

## What went right
- Truck right first build at 40 words, including ten awning stripes, six road dashes, wheels from a note on both.
- Cabins from a count, with colours repeating round the rim, were right once the ring was unfilled.
- Tiles, nav, headings and tapped colours came out right everywhere, colour 97-98 on every page.
- Fixes were quick: most took about a minute or two once the first build had run.
