# n223: items after a count across go in one row over the page, all of them

Found in n221 (round 47). "x3 across: item; item; item" on one short drawn line broke three ways: circus stacked the
items down the page, aquarium squeezed them inside the drawn line and wrapped them, origami printed one of three (and
a fix note listing them again did nothing). Two of those pages then needed fix notes that counted as words.

## Causes found (from what the build was told)
- **Circus:** each item had a comma ("June 4, Opening night"), so the note was read as table rows with cells.
- **Origami, by the build prompt:** the same note was told four ways at once: a table row in its column; "exactly 12
  seats"; and "one centred group, never spread to the page edges".
- **Nothing checked the row.**

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A count across is one row** (`countrow.ts`): "x3 across:", "x3 spaced across:", "3 across:", "row of 3:", "x3 side
  by side:", then exactly that many items. A lower-case part says how they look. A comma in an item makes a main line
  and a smaller line under it; the comma is not printed.
- **Where it goes:** the line says where the row starts and its height. It runs to the line's own end when the line
  covers most of the page, else to the same margin on the other side, or up to the next mark at that height. First
  item starts at the left, last ends at the right, one line each.
- **Rival tells removed:** table rows, sample rows, listed rows on lines, "exactly 12 seats", the centred-group tell and
  the sizing of the line's words all step aside. The check that called the items "directions" and asked for them to be
  removed was wrong too, and is fixed.
- **Checked on the page** (`verifyCountRows`): every item there, in order left to right, at the row's height, ends at
  the span's ends, no wrapping, no count words printed. A miss goes to repair.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n223-count-row.test.ts` (9). Demo: `out-n223/n223-demo.gif` (target, before, after).
