  edge" (meant the progress bar) turned the orange map route grey and ran it off to the right, and turned the
  orange arrival line black: 54 to 47. e3 "4 grey squares under it" (meant the tool column) greyed the four
  coloured thumbnails. e1 "dark middle tab is Week" moved the dark fill to the last tab instead.
- **A fix that changes nothing still reports success**: e3's third run ("thumbnails back to pen colours; delete
  texts 4 grey squares and 5 sliders") edited one icon file, its checks said satisfied, and nothing on the page
  moved.
- **Words in a note printed onto the page**: "4 grey squares below" became a caption under the tool; "5 sliders"
  became a label; e4 sidebar links read "Notifications bold dark" and "Billing stacked" (style words after a list).
- **A note under a short line still lands on the box around it** (12 note moves this half; same as n189):
  header and sidebar notes went to the header bar or sidebar box, headings to "2 lines" with the subtitle.
- **A small drawn ring is built as a diamond** (e5 start dot and driver avatar kept the 4-point shape of the
  drawn ring; the fix filled the dot but kept the diamond).
- **Pen-coloured boxes came out as outlines** until a fix said "fill" (e1 class cards), though a whole note on
  the first run already said "shapes filled in their pen colours".
- **A progress bar is only as long as the orange stroke** (e5: track stopped where the fill line stopped).
