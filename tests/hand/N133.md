# n133: a note listing label-value pairs is a panel of rows, one line each, from just under its heading

Found in n127-n131 (round 39 e4 vet booking): the tall Summary panel was drawn with only a heading line and a button
at its foot, and given one note, "Pet Biscuit, Date Tue 15 Dec, Time 10:30, Vet Dr. Okafor, Fee €45". In 2 of 4
earlier builds each value was stacked under its label (ten lines, not five rows) and the rows began a third of the way
down the panel. Nothing told the build the note's shape, and nothing checked it.

## What was built
- **Label-value notes** (`pairrows.ts` `pairList`): 3-8 comma items, each a capitalised one-word label (two words
  when the second is lower case, "Due date") and a 1-5 word value, labels all different, at least half the values
  holding a digit, currency sign or capital. A comma list of one form (the n130 day row), semicolon legends (n119),
  look words and plain copy ("Home, About, Contact") are left alone.
- **Told to the build**: one row per pair in typed order, label left and value right-aligned on ONE line, the rows
  from right under the panel's heading at a steady pitch, the first row no lower than a y worked out from the drawn
  panel, spare height left empty below the rows.
- **Checked on the page**: each label and value present as two items on one line (never stacked, never run
  together), the value to the label's right, rows in typed order, pitch at most 64px, the first row in the panel's
  top part. A failure names the row and what to change.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 9 unit tests.
