## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A count on a line breaks three different ways** (c1, c2, c4): "x3 across" on one short drawn line stacked the
  items down the page (c1), squeezed all three inside the width of the drawn line (c2), and dropped two of the
  three items (c4, a drawn line nearly full width; a fix note listing them again did nothing). Lead: items after
  "x3 across" are always all printed, in a row spread across the page's width, starting at the drawn line.
- **A fish body with a tail drawn in one stroke became a plain rectangle** (c2): the outline's waist and tail were
  lost, the fill went to its bounding box. The fix note "oval body, triangle tail right" put a dark triangle
  inside the rectangle. Triangles drawn alone stay exact (c4). Lead: a filled freehand outline keeps its outline,
  whatever shape it is.
- **Untapped coloured rings were filled, and one became a tilted square** (c2): two pale ring outlines became a
  filled dot and a rotated square; the fix note "outline ring, no fill" removed both. Lead: an untapped closed ring
  in a pen colour is an outline, and a fix on a shape never removes it.
- **A note naming things in a colour painted the box it landed in** (c5): the first card, tapped white and noted
  "x4 ... red circle ...", came out red while its three copies were white; the fix "white card" repaired it.
- **A filled circle inside a ring landed off centre** (c5): drawn on the same centre, built about 20px up and left;
  "centred in the ring" did not centre it.
- **Dark frames and captions under a repeated card were lost** (c5): "dark frames, captions under" gave frameless
  cards with upper-case captions inside; the bar came out upright.
- **Stripes on a box came with invented shading** (c3): barber stripes were built as shaded 3D bars, and the top
  bar and rows sat 35 to 55px below their drawn lines. Structure 68, layout 51.
- **Stripes note ignored first time** (c1): "3 wide white vertical stripes" on the filled tent was dropped by the
  first build; the fix made them, narrower than asked. A note under two body lines binds only to the second.
- **A note dropped inside a card binds to the card, not the line or box beside it** (c3, c4, c5): four notes had
  to be moved by hand.

## What went right
- Origami triangles exact in place and colour from four outlines and taps (structure 95).
- Serif headline and italic serif from a note; flag, pole and tent outline faithful (c1, c2, c5).
- Barber rows: the x4 list verbatim, gold prices right, rules under, on their own drawn lines.
