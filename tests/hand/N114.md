# n114: a lone ring at the top left of typed rows is row 1's own mark

Found in n162 (round 42 e1 podcast: the hollow ring drawn under "Latest episodes" was built as a filled play button
above row 1, and the rows started under it; e4 dashboard: the grey ring drawn in the "Due soon" card became a dot on
top of row 1's words). Bank home and contacts hit the same thing earlier.

## What was built
- **Ring-led rows** (`openrows.ts`): a rows note typed in open space with no wide field drawn above it, but a lone
  small ring or square (12-56px) drawn above it and to its left, with nothing else drawn and no other note typed
  between: that ring is row 1's mark. The rows start level with it and run from its left edge to the page's right,
  keeping the same margin on the right as the ring keeps from the panel or edge on its left. Pitch spreads the rows
  so the typed note sits about half way down them (40-72px), or ends them above a note typed under them.
- **The mark is on every row** (`rowMarkWords`, used by open rows and by rows in a card, `cardrows.ts`): when a list
  is led by a drawn mark, the build is told it is row 1's own leading mark, repeated at the left of every row in the
  same size, shape and colour, words to its right, and never a separate button or dot above or on the rows.
- A ring in a card now counts as row 1's mark when its middle is below the heading's typed spot (the dashboard's ring
  overlapped the heading's line by a few px and was ignored before).
- **Checked on the page** (`checkRowMarks`): a small near-square box at the mark's x in (nearly) every row's band,
  else the fault names the rows' middles to put the mark on.
- **Replay fix** (`replay.mjs`, `session.mjs`): a toolbar move is now recorded with its spot and replayed; for older
  digests `--bar x,y` gives it. Without it the podcast replay's white tap pressed the bar's mode button (as in n162)
  and every later stroke was lost; that first replay was thrown away.
- 3 unit tests (`n114-row-ring.test.ts`, on the real n162 strokes), n156 and n158 tests updated for the mark check.
  Zero tokens.
