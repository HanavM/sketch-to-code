# n20: a drawn card stays where it was drawn (goal 2026-10-27)

## Built this step
- **A big box gets no big excuse.** The landing check let a box be off by a
  quarter of its own size, so the 422px sign-in card built 90px low "landed".
  The slack now stops at 28px, and a big block that is off only up or down is
  named as centred on the screen, with its drawn top to use instead.
- **Codegen is told a drawn card keeps its spot on the page**: no vertical
  centring (min-h-screen + items-center and friends); its top is the drawn top.
- **Words on a form field fail the check.** A label, heading or line of copy
  newly sitting on an input, textarea or select is a named failure (n13's
  repair squeezed "Password" onto the email field and nothing noticed).
- **A colour the ink stated is never snapped.** The finishing pass rewrote the
  tapped pale grey page (#f3f4f6) to bg-white, so the ink-colour check failed
  every round and the run could never pass (try 2 below: 52.6, page white).
- **A level line drawn for text is checked as the text's middle.** Headings and
  links drawn as lines always failed the region check (a 1px region), so every
  build spent repair rounds it could not win.

Tests: shapemark (2 new), verify (1 new). Build clean; suite 1561 pass, 2 fail
as before (root-only permission tests).
