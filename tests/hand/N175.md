## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A coloured box drawn round one giant letter became a coloured card with the letter knocked out in cream**
  (c4: red-pen box round a big red serif A). One fix removed the square, but the A then filled only about half
  the drawn area. Lead: a box in a colour noted as a letter/word is that glyph in that colour, filling the box;
  never a card. (Same family as n14/n17, which covered black area boxes and outlined words.)
- **A slanted panel edge was built straight** (c5: the dark panel's left edge drawn from x 755 at the top to
  640 at the bottom came out as a vertical split at 640). A whole-sketch fix note to slant it put the slant on
  the wrong block (the light left half went dark); a second fix put it back, still straight. Lead: a filled
  shape with a slanted side keeps that side (clip-path from the drawn corners); a fix about one shape never
  repaints another.
- **Drawn dots built as shaded 3D balls** (c5: three small coloured rings noted "solid holds" came out with a
  glossy gradient; a fix saying "holds flat" did nothing). Same family as open n143 (flat discs unless asked).
- **Single-line text wrapped despite its drawn line** (c1: nav and subtitle each drawn as one line wrapped onto
  two; one whole-sketch fix put both on one line). Lead: a text line is at least as wide as drawn, no wrap.
- **Header and heading sit lower than drawn** (c3: logo and nav 16px low, the centred heading about 30px below
  its line; layout 39). Lead: text on a drawn line is centred on the line, header items on their line.
- **Big stat numbers built small** (c5: "big bold, label under" gave 16px numbers; one fix made them big).
- **A stacked heading's drawn break was moved** (c4: "Printed by hand, / one sheet at a time" broke after
  "one"; a fix note restored it).
- **Middle dots dropped again** (c2 footer: "Tue–Sat · 18:00 and 20:30 · 4 Harbour Lane" built without the dots).
- **Balloons built as rounded rectangles, not ovals** (c1: drawn ellipses became pill shapes with flat sides).

## What went right
- Group note on one balloon reached all three (solid colour, string and basket under each) with no fix.
- Two page taps made the sunset fade at once (c1), and the stepped skyline outline plus a tap was a solid
  skyline with windows (c3).
- A note typed under a heading re-bound the heading note from two lines to one at once (c3, c5).
