# n119: a list note on one of a row of look-alike marks spreads over them

Found in n118 (round 38 e3, budget): the legend was drawn as three small coloured boxes in a column, with one note
"Rent $1,120; Food $700; Travel $520" typed beside the middle one. Only the first box got words; the other two were bare.

## Why, and what was built
- **Small swatches are boxes** (`shapemark.ts`): a box drawn about 14px wide often came out 13.8px after the hand's
  wobble and was dropped as freehand (2 of the 3 swatches). Closed marks now count from 10px, and under 20px a box must
  also fill its square (a tiny ring fills ~79%, so it is not a box) and close within 6px.
- **List notes spread** (`rowwords.ts`): a note split by semicolons or new lines into as many items as the marks in
  the column or row of look-alike marks it is bound to gives each mark one item, in drawn order (top to bottom, or left
  to right). The build is told each item beside its own mark and the page is checked (a label and its amount built as
  two pieces on the mark's row count as its words). Not spread when the counts differ or a mark was typed other words.
- **Items are copy, not directions** (`run2.ts`): the directions check read "Food $700" (after a semicolon) as a
  direction and a repair stripped the amounts (try 2). Spread notes are now left out of the directions brief and check.
- **Words beside a coloured bar stay dark** (`run2.ts` brief): told only, not checked.
