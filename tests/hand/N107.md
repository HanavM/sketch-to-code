## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **Strokes drawn quickly in a row can be lost** (c3): a film frame (box + tap) never appeared, so its tap
  turned the whole strip into a fade; the next box was not a mark yet, so its note *replaced* the note on
  the box beside it ("note updated"). A shape stroke ending on the toolbar was also lost (c1).
- **A tap inside overlapping shapes goes to the back one** (c2 card over its shadow; c1 dark oval under
  the green one): the tilted card's tap made the shadow a yellow-to-navy fade that the build kept even with
  "solid navy shadow" on it. A note can't reach a shape under another shape either.
- **A fix note on a built shape adds new things** (c1): "smooth ovals, dark in front" on the green shape
  said "placed here" and the fix added two new pill shapes (n79 still open).
- **Outline vs fill** (n78 still open): white-pen box with no tap became a solid white button (c3); white
  rings with no tap became solid discs (c4) even with "thin bubbles".
- **Drawn ovals are built as jagged polygons**, and shapes running off the page edge must be closed by hand,
  which is built as a flat cut (c1).
- **Slash/comma copy in one card is scattered** (c4): "Try dive / Half a day, no experience / $95" split
  at the comma too; the lines drawn in the first card weren't used as the layout for all three cards.
- **Size words don't reach**: "giant letter pairs" built at half the cell height (c5); headlines built
  narrower or smaller than their drawn lines (c2, c3).
- Smaller: two page taps made a diagonal fade not top-to-bottom (c4); square buttons built as pills or
  rounded (c1, c2, c5); grid border dropped (c5); text on orange cells turned white (c5); subtitle
  right-aligned (c5); content 10-30px lower than drawn (c3, c4).
