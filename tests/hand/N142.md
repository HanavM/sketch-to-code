# n142: words typed over a full-width divider are a small label above it; a long open polyline is a route line

Found in n139 (round 40 e2 recipe, e3 ride booking, e4 support inbox).

## What was built
- **Rule labels** (`ruleline.ts`, wired in `intent2.ts`, `textplace.ts`, `linespan.ts`, `textrules.ts`, `linkline.ts`, `run2.ts`):
  a level line 300px or longer that runs edge to edge of its column (the column is found from drawn boxes, upright lines and
  the sides of tall boxes such as a sidebar), with its own note typed a little ABOVE it (4-70px, not on it), is a divider with
  a label: the line is a thin rule (kept, no longer refused as "a line that only stands for words"), the band above keeps its
  background, and the words are ordinary 14-18px text just above the rule on the side they were typed. A note asking for big
  type, or a note about the line itself (roads, route, track, border...), keeps the old reading. Told in the brief and
  checked on the page (`rule-labels`: size, above the rule, side); the line-span (heading sized by the line), text-place
  (centring) and text-rules checks leave such lines alone.
- **Route lines** (`routeline.ts`): a stroke that is no plain shape, 300px+ of ink, open (ends 150px+ apart), simplifying to
  3-10 corners whose long runs are level or plumb, is a ROUTE: one SVG path through its points, in its pen colour (dark if the
  pen is white), about 5px, round joins, over what it crosses. Its pen colour no longer fills the box round it. Checked on the
  page (`routes`: an SVG element covers its extent). Mountain ridges, waves and chart lines (slanted runs) are not routes.
- **A note about the line is never a heading** (`linespan.ts` `ABOUT_LINE`): "white roads, thick" typed on a road line had
  been built as a giant "white roads" heading.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 12 unit tests (`packages/pipeline/tests/n142-rule-route.test.ts`).
