## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A full-width coloured band at the bottom became a footer with made-up text** (c1: the blue sea band with
  pale stripes was built as a white footer: name, links, email, copyright). The first fix note bound to one
  inner div (stripes turned diagonal); a whole-sketch note turned the band blue but the invented text stayed.
  Lead: a bare filled box with only lines in it is a picture band, never a footer; nothing is invented in it.
- **Style words printed as heading text when the copy has its own commas** (c3: "First tracks, / warm fire,
  huge bold, right" built a third line "huge bold"; the heading also took the subtitle's line and sat on top of it).
  One fix note removed it. Lead: trailing style words (huge, bold, italic, right) are never copy; the stack is
  the lines drawn, not the next line down.
- **A group note on one of three like rings reached only that one** (c2: "solid donuts: edge, icing, hole" on the
  middle donut; the outer two were built as squared-off, clipped shapes; layout 43). Same family as open n132.
- **A note on one of six drawn pins reached only that pin** (c5: "six solid white pins" made one solid pin; the
  other five white-pen boxes were outlines, per the white-on-dark rule). One fix made all six solid.
- **Middle dots dropped from a subtitle** (c5: "Tuesday league · 8 lanes · shoes included" built without the dots).
- **Thick outline button built filled** (c4: "Book tickets, thick outline" became a solid black button in caps;
  one fix made it outlined).
- **Stat row centred and lifted** (c3: three stats drawn at left at one height came out centred under each line,
  about 45px higher than drawn).
