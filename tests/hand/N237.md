## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A count with too few items invents the rest** (c3): "x6 across: QWERTY" built six rings, the first reading
  QWERTY and the other five RIBBON, SPOOL, LEVER, CARRIAGE, PLATEN, none of them typed. Same for ASDFG. Then a fix
  listing the letters did not replace them: the rows grew to 11 and 9 rings squeezed together. A third note had to
  delete them. Lead: never print a word nobody typed; items after a count replace, never append.
- **Shapes drawn later were built behind shapes drawn earlier** (c4): cars drawn after the mountains ended up behind
  both, two of three hidden. A first fix ("in front of mountains") did nothing. The same note also asked for flat
  fills and also did nothing. Lead: drawing order is stacking order.
- **Invented shading** (c4): both mountains got a darker right half, nobody asked.
- **A note on one line changed another line** (c4): "thick rail" made the rail thick and also put a thick black line
  down the big mountain's slanted side.
- **A group note overrode a tap** (c4): "each car: 2 white windows, 2 black wheels" gave all cars right, but the
  yellow-tapped third came out red.
- **A page tap under a later shape was taken by that shape** (c5): the stage stayed white and the beam got the page
  colour as a fade. Lead: a tap made before the shape existed is the page's.
- **A drawn, tapped, noted button was never built** (c5): the gold pill was lost on the first build and through two
  fixes that named it; nothing said so.
- **Style words with a count were printed** (c5): "x8 vertical stripes, alternate darker" was built right AND printed
  in capitals on both curtains. The heading's second drawn line also became an underline.
- **Fixes broke things that were right** (c5): "flat greyish beam" turned the drawn beam into a box; the next fix did
  not restore it and instead removed the puppet's head and colours, leaving a dark shape. The page ended worse to the
  eye than after the fix before it, and there is no way back from a bad fix short of starting over.

## What went right
- Pinwheel and cactus right first build with no fix at 41 and 31 words: triangles and one-stroke outlines exact, hub,
  stick, pots and bands in the tapped colours.
- Ring pairs: drawing the first and last ring of a row spaced the row correctly once the words were sorted.
- Stripes from a count and a colour on a curtain were right first time (apart from the printed words).
- Every heading came out on its drawn line and at its drawn width.
