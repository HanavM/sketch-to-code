# n30: colour taps and colour notes reach everything

Gaps from round 31 e4 weather (n28, n29): the sun came out a grey lopsided blob, the page fade was never
built, and panel text came out dark despite "all text white".

## What was built
- **A tap under the toolbar reaches the sketch.** A colour tap on the bar's bare surface (not a button,
  not the grip) goes through to the page under it. The weather page's second page tap (bottom, for the
  fade) sat under the bar and was lost, so the page was one flat colour; now the fade shows on the
  sketch at once and is built.
- **A ring coloured in with a tap is a solid disc in exactly that colour.** Even when the reading calls
  it a picture (a sun), a tapped ring is never traced as a path: one round element exactly where and as
  big as drawn, the tapped hex, opaque. A ring inside another sits on top of it, so the outer shows as a
  halo. Checked on the page (tight colour match). Skipped when any note asks for glows or blur, so soft
  glows stay soft.
- **"All text white" means every word.** The note is read, told to the build as one rule for every
  panel, row and caption, and checked: at least 85% of text elements must be that colour. The
  readability check no longer "fixes" that text to dark.
