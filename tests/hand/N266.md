  preview too. "page flat navy" was ignored until a separate fix, and the stars landed in other places (the page has 11, I tapped 6).
- **Run failed on a whole-page note** (rocket): "whole page background flat navy, no fade, no orange" gave "couldn't turn
  that into actions (design action with neither container nor strokes)" after 243 s. Three words on the page worked.
- **A style note is printed as content** (library): "four covers solid" replaced the title The Deep Blue, surviving a fix.
- **Words typed under a drawn box go inside it, and a box loses its drawn size** (library).
- **A small fix repaints a large thing** (watchmaker): "hub gold" on the hub made the face gold, and "hub round dot" made the hub navy; "button hollow" dropped the outline.
- **One note about all the tracks reached one** (mixer): "tracks thick black" went to Bass only.
- **deleteNote can't find a note** typed earlier (library): my delete was refused with "no such note"; not traced.
