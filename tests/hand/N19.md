## Gap found and built this step
- **A heading took the subtitle's second line.** The sunset heading is two
  drawn lines (long, then short "hour"); the subtitle under it is two tight
  lines. The heading stack jumped over the subtitle's first line and took its
  short wrap line as heading line 3, so the heading broke into three rows and
  sat on top of the subtitle, and the subtitle's note left "little longer." out.
  Now:
  - a heading stack never jumps a line in its way, and never takes a line held
    tight by a neighbour (a paragraph's wrap);
  - a note on a paragraph's first line covers its wrap lines (level lines
    stepped under 36px apart, overlapping, no longer).
  Shown at once on the sketch: the heading note now says "on these 2 lines",
  the subtitle note "on these 2 lines". Result: three rows -> two, build 522 s
  -> 132 s, tokens 97k -> 36k, likeness 72.1 -> 76.4.
