# n170: a stalled repair is retried fresh however few its problems; a big heading on a line is sized right the first time

Found in n165 try 1 (round 42 e5 gradebook): the repair sat silent for 152 s (no tokens at all) with 8 checks failing,
and the run kept the build as it was. The n169 retry only fired with 4+ failures, and only when a full-length repair
still fitted the 12-minute cap; the stalled turn's own 165 s made a retry look too long. Also in n165 (and n168): the
heading drawn as a 210px line ("Year 9 Science big bold") was built 261px long and only fixed by a repair, because a
note with a size word was never told the font-size its line implies.

## What was built
- **Any no-edit stall gets one fresh retry** (`run2.ts`, `repair-time.ts` `freshRepairFits`): silent stalls and
  reads-without-an-edit alike, whatever the failure count. The retry is short on purpose: a new agent, the biggest 3
  problems (or all, when fewer than 4), asked for a first Edit within a minute and held to 90 s without one. It fits
  when 90 s plus the verify margin are left, not the stalled turn's length.
- **A big heading on a line 200px or longer is told its size up front** (`textplace.ts`): the size word no longer skips
  the line-sets-its-size brief (shorter lines stay with the title cap). The font-size counts every letter shown,
  one-letter words and digits too (`linespan.ts` `spanFontPx`: "Year 9 Science" is 14 letters, not 12), and a heading
  whose note does not say bold is not told bold.
- 5 unit tests (`n170-stall-retry-heading.test.ts`, the real gradebook line and the real n165 try 1 timings). Zero tokens.
