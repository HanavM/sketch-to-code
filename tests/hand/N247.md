# n247: a page sketched inside the screen stays inside it

Found in n244 (wifi, try 2). Everything was drawn inside the 900px screen and every box was built within 5px of its spot, but the page wrapper kept 40px of padding under the last button. The document was 918px tall, so the page could scroll by 18px, and it came back from the reload scrolled to its end, with every part higher than drawn. All checks passed, because none looked at the page's height. From what kept runs report, about 11 of 81 recent builds ran past the screen (904 to 960px). That count is rough: it comes from the checked boxes, not from a page height.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **The page's own height is now measured.** Each page snapshot carries the document's full size.
- **The build is told:** when everything drawn ends inside the screen, the page does not scroll, page padding goes on the top and sides only, and no footer or rows get added.
- **The check names which of two faults it is,** so a repair gets the right job: empty room under the content (remove it, move nothing), or one part a little off the bottom (take the height out of the space above it, keep the drawn sizes).
- **Never held to it:** a page drawn past the screen, a note asking for a long or scrolling page, a count that reaches past the screen, a page that already ran past before a fix, and an overrun of real content by more than 80px (the placement checks name those part by part).
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n247-fits-screen.test.ts` (13). Demo: `out-n247/n247-demo.gif`.

## Did it help
- **What was asked for, yes, in 3 builds of 3:** each page ended at the screen's edge, and none came back scrolled. Three builds is a small sample.
- **Likeness on wifi, no clear gain:** 64.2 and 56.0 became 60.1 and 57.3. The mean moved from 60.1 to 58.7, within the spread between tries. n244 guessed the scroll cost part of try 2's 8 points; these runs show little of it, as a page with no scroll scored about the same. Layout stays at 29-38 with every part looking right, and I did not trace why.
- **Budget rose 89.5 to 93.6 on one build each,** with no repair and the same tokens, and its layout score rose 76 to 87. Some of that may be chance: this page has ranged 79-89 between builds before.

## Not seen, or still off
- **The check's failure path has not been seen firing live.** The telling alone may have done the work: budget had no repair at all. On wifi, each build took two repairs (n244 took one and two), and the run records don't say which failures sent them, so the check may have sent some. Its unit tests pass, and the page's height is confirmed in the overlay code the app actually serves, but no live build has failed it.
- **Wifi's repairs cost about 58k tokens each try,** and a colour check still failed after them: try 1 on the chart's card, try 2 with "nothing rendered near the drawing" for the button's dark colour, though that colour is on the page. These look false; I did not confirm it. New node: n249.
- **"412 Mbps" still loses its Mbps** in the big number (n246), **and all six icons are orange,** as told.
- **The page does not come back from a reload at its top** when it really is long. That behaviour is unchanged; only the small overrun that caused it here is gone.
