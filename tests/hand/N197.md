## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **Drawn text lines pasted into the page as coloured bars** (c3): the two red headline lines came out as red
  bars above and under each line of the headline, and the words were dark, not the pen colour. A fix made the
  words red but turned the bars green and kept them. Lead: a pen line under a heading note is where the words
  go (their colour), never a rule, unless a note says so.
- **Untapped ring built as a filled disc with invented spokes** (c4 wheels): two black outline rings came out
  as solid black discs clipped top and bottom, with yellow spokes; a fix made them rings but still clipped
  by a box narrower than the drawn ring. Same family as n194 (no tap = outline) but for rings, and n143
  (invented detail).
- **Tapped inner ring took the outer ring's colour** (c2): the cream clock face, tapped cream inside a gold
  outline ring, was built gold; fixed by a note. A gold outline button was built filled; the fix then
  dropped its border entirely (same as r44 c3).
- **Notes under nav lines bound to a 2-line group with the header divider** (c2): all three nav notes went to
  one band made of the nav line and the full-width divider under it; dragged by hand (3 moves).
- **A tap inside overlapping shapes filled the wrong one** (c1): the front dune's tap lay inside three
  shapes and the front one stayed unfilled on the sketch; a note fixed it in the build.
- **Curves drawn with a few corner points built as sharp polygons** (c1 dunes, c5 flames as diamonds):
  a person drawing a hill or a flame expects the smooth shape.
- **Text drawn from a line's left end started indented** (c2 kicker and body about 30-45px right of the drawn
  start); body text small. Nav words run together (c3).

## What went right
- Two page taps gave the warm sky gradient (c1); flat sun disc and three dunes in pen colours.
- Candles, shelf and glowing flames from 18 strokes and 35 words, no fix (c5, 88.2).
- Card trio with title, line and price from one note each, borders kept (c4).
