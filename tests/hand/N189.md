# n189: a note typed just beside a short line is that line's words

Found sketching round 44 c1-c5 (n187): 8 notes had to be dragged onto the right mark before Run. A note typed
16-25px under or over its short line bound to something bigger nearby instead: the band round three stat lines
(c2, three times), the panel round a heading and its subtitle (c3, twice), a sun ring drawn later near the nav
line (c4), the colour block under the logo line (c5), the button beside a date line (c1).

## What was built
- **The nearest line wins.** Typed outside every mark, when the nearest mark is a short line (within 28px),
  boxes, rings and shapes a little further off leave the note (c1 date vs button, c5 logo 18px vs block 19px).
- **A line inside a band or panel takes the note typed beside it**, not the band or panel round it (c2 stats,
  c3 heading and subtitle). A note that names an area (panel, band, card, box...) or lists several short items
  ("Profile, Account, Notifications, Billing") still goes to the area.
- **Short lines count.** A stat's 36px line is a line (text lines needed 40px), so "12" and "40" find it.
- **A line seeds the note over a newer mark further off.** The nav note typed 17px under the nav line used to
  seed on the sun ring drawn half a minute later 43px away, and the nav line was not even in its group (c4).
- **A list row leaves the next row's line** once that line is clearly further and has its own note (c5 rows,
  each row note took two rows).
- **Two lines drawn on one spot are a bar, not a text line** (a progress track and its fill), so labels typed over
  a progress bar stay off it (round 42 quiz).
- Bench: `tests/hand/bindcheck.mjs` replays a page's strokes and notes at their recorded pace and counts the
  notes that land off the mark they were typed for (`n189/expected.json`); session op `bound`.
