# n45: a one-line heading spans its drawn line; a pointed pill is a pill; a subtitle is not a heading's wrap

## What was built
- **A one-line heading is checked against its drawn line** (packages/pipeline/src/linespan.ts, wired into the
  build's checks as `line-span`). A level line whose note asks for big type (big, huge, heading, title...) is found
  on the page by its copy words (style words taken out); if its letters run under 85% or over 118% of the drawn
  length, the repair is told the font-size that makes it span, to keep it on ONE line (nowrap) and keep its block
  height so nothing under it moves. A heading drawn as one line that wrapped onto two fails too. Lines that are
  part of a drawn multi-line stack are left to the stack check. Zero tokens.
- **A wide shape with flat top and bottom and bulging ends is a pill** (shapemark.ts `asPill`): a button drawn
  with pointed ends (the way a pill is drawn with a mouse) was freehand and built as a hexagon. It is now a box
  flagged `pill`, told "border-radius 9999px, never a hexagon", and never traced. Wide rings stay rings (tested
  first), square boxes stay boxes.
- **A line with a sentence of its own is not a heading's wrap** (ink textmark.ts `lineStacks`; notes now carry
  their text to the stack reader, in the overlay and the build). Bookshop's subtitle line, a little shorter than
  the heading line and on the heading pitch, was read as the heading's second line in 2 of 4 runs and built as a
  wrapped heading. A note of 6+ words typed on the lower line (not the note above) now ends the heading.
- Unit tests: packages/pipeline/tests/linespan-pill.test.ts (12), packages/ink/tests/ownsentence.test.ts (3).

## Gaps found
- Nav words typed round a centred logo ("Shop Events, Leaf & Spine..., Clubs Visit" on one short centred line) are
  spread to the page edges in 3 of 4 runs. The line is 524px and centred: the row should stay within it.
- The heading/subtitle stack misread came from the reading grouping both lines into one action; one action per
  line is not guaranteed, so stack rules must not depend on it (now they do not, for sentences).
