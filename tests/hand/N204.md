# n204: a big ring drawn in a black, grey or white pen with no tap is an outline ring

Found in n197 (round 45 c4, bike repair): the two wheels were drawn as black rings with no tap. Every build made black
discs with yellow spokes, cut flat at the top and bottom; the fix note "thick black outline rings only, no fill or
spokes" made rings but cut their sides (80.6 with that fix, 66 words). Round 45 e3 (audio mixer): the grey knob rings
came out filled discs too.

## Why it happened
- A ring drawn in a pen with no tap fell through to "filled with the pen colour". Only small rings (up to 40px, n146)
  and rings noted as an arc or track (n129) were kept hollow.
- The bike rings sat in a picture the reader called a bicycle, so they were traced as paths and the build drew its own
  wheel in an SVG too small for it: the circle was clipped by its own box.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Untapped neutral ring = outline ring** (`outlineRingPen` in `hollowring.ts`): a ring 44px or more, about round, in
  a writing pen (black, grey, white; not a hue, not a tinted pale pen), no tap, and no note asking for a disc, dot,
  picture or avatar ("no fill or spokes" does not count as asking). Never traced, even inside a picture.
- **Told plainly**: the drawn line only, the drawn size, a line about 6% of its size, inside transparent, nothing added
  inside (no spokes, hub, pattern), the whole ring visible (a bordered round div, or an SVG whose viewBox holds it all).
- **Checked on the page** (`outlineRingBuilt`): a pen-coloured disc there fails; an SVG circle that runs past its own
  SVG box (cut flat) fails; nothing round of that colour and size there fails.
- A list's leading ring built this way repeats hollow on every row (`hollowRowMarks`).
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n204-outline-rings.test.ts` (4); n184's "dark ring in a picture is traced" test now
  expects an outline ring.
