## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **Starting a stroke on a note chip silently deletes or swallows it** (e2: two strokes began where a
  just-typed note's chip sat; the first press hit "Will you come?"'s close button and deleted it, the second
  hit "Maybe"'s; neither box was drawn and nothing said so). Had to re-type both notes and redraw from another
  corner. Lead: while drawing, a pen press on a chip draws (or chips step aside from the pen), and a delete
  says what it deleted. Sketching all strokes first and notes last avoided it on e3-e5.
- **A tap inside a tiny box (a checkbox) became a page tap and made a page fade** (e4: orange tap in an 18px
  box plus the white page tap = orange-to-white page gradient). Undone. Lead: a tap inside any closed mark
  fills that mark, however small.
- **A label-and-placeholder note was printed literally** (e2: "Your name above, Full name inside" built as
  that whole text inside the input; a whole-sketch fix moved it above but still printed the phrase). Lead:
  "X above, Y inside" on an input is a label and a placeholder.
- **A heading line grouped with nearby lines into a huge stacked heading** (e5: the progress line inside the
  video, the heading line and the meta line were bound as one 3-line heading; built at about 64px over two
  lines). One fix made it one line. Lead: lines in different pen colours, or inside another box, never stack
  into one heading.
- **A row note in commas not split into the drawn table columns** (e3: "Bitcoin bold, 0.21 BTC, $14,120, +2.4%
  green" printed "Bitcoin bold, 0.21 BTC, $14,120" as the name; only the % went right). Lead: comma parts of a
  row note fill the header's columns in order; style words are never printed.
- **Tap in a card corner built as a grey circle icon in every card** (e1, first build; a fix removed them).
- **Grid built about 20px below the drawn cells** (e1: layout 64), and middle dots dropped again (e2, e5).
- **Heading drawn at x 330 built at x 380** (e4).
