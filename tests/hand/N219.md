# n219: a drawn pattern stays flat in its named colours; a big box among the strokes is a column edge

Found in n218 (round 41 c4, chess night). "checkerboard: black and cream squares, 4 across, 7 down" came back with its cream squares as grey
fades in one build of two (layout 59, likeness 74.9), and nothing checked it.

## Causes found
- **Nothing told or checked a pattern's colours.** Two colour words on a repeating pattern reached the build only as words.
- **The build was told two opposite things.** The panel is drawn in black pen, so the build was told to fill the whole block #151515 "at full
  strength, never fade it", next to a note asking for black and cream squares. A likely reason for see-through cream over black; I did not catch
  the grey build's own code, so this is not proven.
- **Replaying showed a second fault: the date was told to go right-aligned.** The panel was read as a drawn outline, not a box, so no action held a
  box and the date's line (x 606 to 826) was measured against the whole page. Told right-aligned, it came out wrapped and centred, then far right.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Pattern colours told and checked.** A note naming 2+ colours for a pattern (checkerboard, stripes, squares, tiles, polka dots, a pattern): every
  cell one flat colour, using the sketch's own ink when it matches. Checked on the page: faded, see-through or off-colour cells, a wash laid over
  them, a named colour nowhere on it, and patterns built as one gradient. A miss goes to repair.
- **The box under a pattern takes no one fill**, in the brief and in the ink-colour check.
- **A big box among all the strokes is a column edge**, whichever action holds it, in the brief and in the page check.
- **A heading's slash note is no longer read as table rows.** It was failing a check on every chess build with "the typed row MATE huge bold caps is not on the page".

## Did it help
- **No grey in 4 builds of 4**, against 1 grey in 2 before. Small sample; the page has been seen, so none of this counts toward done.

## Still off, new leads
- The panel was given an invented shadow in both final builds ("flat panel" only covers drawn boxes, and this one was read as an outline).
- The panel keeps its pen outline as a thin grey frame in the cream cells.
- The "build around the drawn paths (fills, interior detail, shading)" wording in the build prompt and code comment invites shading on anything read
  as a depiction, which may also be behind invented shading seen on other pages. Left alone: changing it affects every drawing and needs its own test.
- In one earlier build the date line's check judged the MATE heading instead (122px off), a false failure. Not seen in the last two.
