# n73: a typed time is plain text; a note's look words and directions never show on the page

Replayed the n66 sketches of round 34 e2 (departures: 11 strokes, 9 notes) and e3 (habits: 16 strokes, 12 notes)
word for word: first build only, no fix notes, no note drags.

## Gap (from n71)
- **Departures:** "14:32, big bold white" was built as a live clock on every try (it showed the real time).
- **Habits:** "Read 20 pages, bold; done M T W F" on each card: the build printed the raw note under the title as a
  grey subtitle (look word and the "done ..." direction shown as copy).
- Found while replaying habits: a repair round that fixed one check printed two cards' directions as their titles
  ("Drink water; done M-S, not Sun"); the share of checks passing stayed the same, so the repair was kept.

## What was built
- **Typed copy vs directions** (`typedcopy.ts`): a note is split into its copy (before a trailing look tail like
  ", big bold white", and before the first semicolon), its look words and its directions (after a semicolon). The
  build is told only the copy is text, and checked that no direction, multi-word look tail or raw note is printed.
- **A typed time is static** (`typedcopy.ts`): times typed in a note's copy (14:32, 9:05 pm) are told to be shown
  exactly as typed, never a live clock or timer; checked on the page.
- **A repair that breaks what was working is undone** (`run2.ts`): if a repair turn makes a passing check fail and
  the share passing does not rise, the page is put back as it was before that turn.
