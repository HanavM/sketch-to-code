# n269: scattered taps on the bare page are dots, and a flat page stays flat

Found by the n266 redraw of the rocket page (r46 c1): six white star taps and an amber flame tap made the page a white and orange fade, in the preview and the build, the headline vanished, and the fix note "whole page background flat navy, no fade, no orange" then failed its Run.

## What actually went wrong
- Every coloured tap on bare page went into one page fade, however many there were. Six white taps said "fade to white".
- The flame was drawn as an open U, so its amber tap counted as a page tap too. That is where the orange came from.
- A look for the whole page has no ink and no block, and the reading refused such an action ("neither container nor strokes"), which ended the Run.
- A shape filled as an svg path failed the colour check, as that check only read CSS backgrounds. The repair it asked for painted over good work: try 1's rocket body was cut to a sliver, try 2's flame got a square block behind it.

## What was built
- **Three or more taps in one colour, scattered on the bare page beside a tap of another colour, are small dots where they landed.** The preview shows them at once and the page keeps its colour. Told to the build and checked on the page. A ring round one tap stays a spotlight; two or three different colours stay a fade; taps of only one colour still colour the page.
- **A tap between the arms of an open mark, in its own pen, is that mark's**, not the page's.
- **"page flat navy" wins over a fade**: a note about the page itself saying flat, solid, plain or no fade. "flat icons" and "solid button" say nothing here.
- **A whole-page look goes on the page itself** and never fails a Run. An action with no ink, no block and no word about the page is still refused.
- **A colour painted as an svg shape counts** in the colour check.
- Across 211 kept sketches, 176 with page taps, the dot rule changes one page: this one (`n269scan.mjs`).
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n269-page-dots.test.ts` (12).
