# n12: a tapped colour keeps its strength (goal 2026-10-27)

## Built this step
- **Codegen is told full strength.** Every colour the ink fills a mark with is
  now asked for at opacity 1 with the exact colour in the block's middle; a
  glow or blurred shape may soften only its edge, with a blur cap from its
  drawn size (about a quarter of its width), and never fade through opacity,
  a see-through colour or a paler tint.
- **The colour check measures strength, not just the hex.** n10's glows had
  the exact hex but showed about half of it (pink painted rgb(133,49,113) in
  the middle) and passed. The check now multiplies the element's and every
  parent's opacity, the colour's own alpha and how much a blur of that size
  leaves in the middle; under 80% is a named failure with the blur to use.

Tests: inkcolour (1 new: faded, faded wrapper, see-through, soft edge passes,
brief). Build clean; suite 1562 pass, 2 fail as before (root-only tests).
