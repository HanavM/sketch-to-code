## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A closed triangle is read as a circle and built as a rectangle** (c5): both mountains came out as
  boxes (the stroke stats even count them as circles). A fix note "both mountains are triangles" painted
  extra triangles over the boxes and the page got worse; the worse fix was kept. Biggest new gap:
  closed polygons (triangles, waves, hills) should be built from their drawn outline (clip-path).
- **Tap colours land on the wrong region when a curve splits a box** (c1): the two sky-blue taps above the
  wave and the pale tap below it were swapped (sky pale, wave blue fade). A fix swapped them but the fade
  ran the wrong way (dark at top, light at bottom, opposite to the taps).
- **A tilted box with a divider and two fills was dropped entirely by the first build** (c3): only its text
  was built, floating on the page; an invented "12" appeared beside the heading. A fix note brought the
  ticket back (78.1); the heading stayed on one line and the stub text sat centred, not where drawn.
- **Outlined giant type came out filled, and below its drawn lines** (c2): the note said "giant outlined
  letters"; built solid white, letters' bottoms ~100px under the drawn lines. "WORK, same" did not copy
  the style; each line needed its own fix. Red underline under the link was dropped.
- **A note typed just above a two-line paragraph also grabs the heading line above it** (c1, c4): had to
  drag the note onto the paragraph each time.
- **A note typed in open space is "placed here" and still can't be dragged onto a mark** (c2; known since
  n35): dropping it gives "whole sketch"; had to delete and retype.
- **Two far-apart heading lines (250px) were not one heading** (c2): the note took only the first line.
- **A centred heading built much narrower than its drawn line** (c4, known n46/n47): layout 0.31 on a page
  that looks right otherwise.
- **The page colour from a tap is not shown on the sketch** (c2, black page).
