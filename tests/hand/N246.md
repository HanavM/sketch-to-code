# n246: short typed words stay as typed, and a sentence beside a box stays beside it

Found in n238 (round 48). "8 to 2" on a shift bar came out a big 8 over a small "to 2", and a fix did not change it (rota). "Download now, 412 Mbps" lost its Mbps and put the number first (wifi). "Friday was your shortest night, 4 hours 20." typed 30px right of a button was bound to that button, read as a stat, and "Set a bedtime" vanished (sleep).

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A range is words.** "8 to 2", "10 to 4", "9am to 5pm" are never a stat card.
- **A stat keeps its words in typed order.** Words typed before the number read above it, also inside one note. Mbps, Gbps, kbps, ms, fps, mph, kph, kWh, kW and KB are units, so they stay on the value.
- **A value with a unit stays on one line.** Its size now also answers to the card's width, the build is told to keep it on one line, and a wrapped value is caught on the page. Found in this step: "38 Mbps" wrapped and pushed its label over the card's top.
- **A sentence beside a box stays beside it.** At least 4 words, ending in . ! or ?, typed more than 12px outside the box: text at that spot, on no mark. The sketch's bar says so ("here, as text"). A short note, or one that opens with an order ("Make this bigger."), still binds to the mark, and so does anything typed on a line.
- **A sentence with a comma is no stat,** whatever else is on the box.
- **A short label stays at the end of the bar it was typed at.** Found in this step. On a bar at least 280px wide, one note of up to 5 words with no comma, typed in the outer quarter: told and checked. A button under 280px wide stays centred.
- **Look words on a list of names are never printed.** Found in this step: "Drift bold, Week, Month, Goals right" printed "Drift bold" and "Goals right". Names are told without them, printing them is caught on the page, and "right" on the last name puts the names after the first at the bar's right end.
- **Replay keeps a drag made before the first Run.** The session now records what moved. Older digests lack this, so I backfilled the one rota drag by hand, from where the n238 picture shows that chip. An earlier replay without it (rota try 1) put the hours on the first bar.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n246-typed-order.test.ts` (17). Demo: `out-n246/n246-demo.gif`.

## Did it help
- **What it set out to fix, yes, on the builds with each rule in:** rota bars 2 builds of 2 on one line, wifi stats 1 of 1 with both right, sleep's button and sentence 2 of 2, sleep's names 1 of 1. Each is a small sample. No fix note was needed for any of them.
- **Words: no real drop to claim.** The counts here (rota 66, sleep 55, wifi 73) are the same first notes as before, with the fix notes left out of the replay. These faults no longer need a fix note: on rota that saves "all bars: words on one line", 6 of its 84 words; on sleep, part of one fix note. Rota's grid lines and split footer would still need theirs, and sleep's fade and ring still need theirs (n245).
- **Likeness barely moved.** Rota stayed 72-73 with the page now looking nearly the same as the target. Wifi's 64.7 is its best first build but within its spread (56-64). Sleep's 62.9 is a first build with no fix, so it can't be set against 85 after two fixes; its fade costs most of it (colour 82, structure 58).
- **Rota's layout score was 33 in all four builds,** even with the bars in their exact spots. I did not trace why. That is the same question as wifi's, so n249 now covers both, and it looks like the most useful next step.

## Not seen, or still off
- **The wrapped-value and bar-end checks have not been seen firing live.** Unit tests only; the telling was enough in these builds.
- **"right" on the last name was only seen working once with the rule in** (sleep try 2). Rota try 2 also put all three names at the right without the rule, and rota try 1 did not, so builds vary here.
- **Rows' names start at the page's edge (x 2) in rota try 2,** though the note was typed at x 40. New node n250.
- **The footer "6 people, 42 hours, Cost $780" stays one line.** That is what was typed; the target has it split. A sketcher can type two notes.
- **The sentence rule depends on a full stop.** A sentence typed with no ending stop beside a box still binds to the box.
