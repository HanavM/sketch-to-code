# n51: a shape drawn as its outline is built as that outline; taps colour the side of a curve they sit on

Round 33 (n49) lost most on two pages for one reason: shapes drawn as outlines came back as boxes, and
colour taps ignored the curve that split a panel.

## What was built
- **Closed outlines with a few sharp corners are shapes** (`packages/pipeline/src/outline.ts`): a closed
  stroke that is not a box, ring or pill and simplifies to 3-8 sides with at least two hard corners (a
  triangle, a mountain) is one element at its drawn spot and size, clipped to its outline
  (`clip-path: polygon(...)`, points from the ink). Smooth blobs (glows) are left alone. Told to codegen in a
  new SHAPES DRAWN AS OUTLINES block and checked on the page: some element about that size, near the spot,
  whose clip-path covers the drawn outline (overlap 70% or more); a plain box there fails with the exact
  clip-path to use.
- **A curve across a box cuts it in two** (`packages/ink/src/inkfill.ts` `splitRegions`): a bowed stroke
  whose ends sit on the edges of a drawn box (or of the page) splits it; the smaller side is the shape it
  draws (a wave, a hill), closed along the box's edge. It is built and checked like an outline.
- **Taps go to the side of the curve they sit on**, and **two taps far apart on one mark make a fade** from
  the first towards the last (closer than 48px is still a re-colouring). The fade is told as an exact
  linear-gradient and checked: every stop present and running the way it was tapped (a reversed fade fails).
  This also closes the round 31 e5 gap (two taps on a banner kept only the last colour).
- **The overlay shows it at once**: the cut-off side is painted in its own outline and a two-tap mark shows
  its fade the moment the pen lifts; the status line says "filled the part this curve cuts off".
- Snapshots keep up to 1600 characters of an element's clip-path (was 160), so outlines can be checked.
