# n29: notes land where typed

Gaps from round 31 (n28): notes on the inbox and weather pages went to the wrong place.

## What was built
- **A colour tap never decides what a note is about.** Taps were the newest strokes, so a note typed
  in a small box next to a tap seeded on the taps, the thread dropped them, and the note fell to the
  whole sketch (e2 Compose, Inbox 12). Taps are now skipped when a note looks for its mark.
- **Words typed in empty space keep their spot.** A note binds to a mark only within 48px (was 120).
  Further off it is placed: codegen is told where it was typed and the page is checked (words within
  about 48px of that height). e4: "Lisbon" no longer binds to the sun, the date stays top right;
  e2: the sender line sits beside the avatar.
- **A drawn divider is a column edge.** A level line just right of an upright line is left-aligned in
  its column, even when no action cites the divider. e2: the reading-pane title was told right-aligned
  (measured against the page); now left at x 664.
