# n177: one giant letter fills its box; a slanted panel keeps its slant

Found in n175 (round 43 c4 letterpress, c5 climbing gym).

## What was built
- **One named letter is a big word.** "giant red serif letter A filling the box" matched none of the big-word words
  (only "letters", plural), so the red-pen box was not an area: it became a red card with the A knocked out in cream.
  `areas.ts`: letter / initial / monogram / glyph / character count as a word, and `bigWordOf` reads "letter A" as A.
  The box is then an area (no card), the pen colour is the letter's colour (n140), and the letter must fill the box.
- **A single letter is checked by its font-size.** A 320px serif A drew a 230px letter in a 431px box and passed on its
  line box; the next run's 599px A failed as "spilling" on its 689px line box. For one or two characters the check now
  compares the font-size with the size that fills the box (within 0.8x-1.3x), not the line box.
- **A slanted side is kept.** The climbing gym's dark panel, drawn (755,2) → right edge → (640,898), passed the upright
  box test (its corner tolerance grows with size) and was built as a straight split at 640. `outline.ts` `slantedQuad`:
  a big closed four-corner stroke with a bounding-box corner left 40px+ (and 10%+) empty, and two corners hit closely,
  is that slanted shape: built clipped to the drawn outline, checked by the outline check. Rough or round-cornered
  boxes (all corners a little off) stay boxes.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n177-letter-and-slant.test.ts` (7).
