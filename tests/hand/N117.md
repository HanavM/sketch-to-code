# n117: a button drawn at the bottom of a tall panel stays there

Found in n116 (round 38 e1, hotel, try 2): the calendar panel was drawn as a tall box with the Reserve button drawn as a
smaller box at its bottom. The build followed "a calendar drawn that tall fills it", stretched the month's rows to the
panel's height (~58px a row against the target's ~49) and pushed the button ~30px below where it was drawn.

## Built
- **Panel feet** (`panelfoot.ts`): from the drawing alone, a box at least 200px tall holding one smaller box (at least
  half its width, at most 90px tall) in its lower 40%, with nothing else drawn down there, is a panel with a foot. The
  build is told the foot's top and height in px (pinned: flex column, margin-top:auto) and that a calendar, list or grid
  above it keeps natural rows and never stretches; this overrides the "drawn that tall fills it" rule for those boxes.
  Checked on the page: the foot within 12px of its drawn top, and a number grid above it with rows no taller than 54px.
  Zero tokens.
- **Gap under the top bands** (`topbands.ts`): the build is now told where the first thing drawn under the last band
  starts, and to leave exactly that gap (hotel: 44px under the search bar), not a default section padding.
