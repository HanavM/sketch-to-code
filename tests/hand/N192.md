# n192: "skip" strikes a wrong step from the plan before Go

Found replaying round 44 e3 photo editor (n188 strokes, pens, notes and fix notes; fix plan held). The fix note
"delete texts 4 grey squares and 5 sliders" meant the words of two notes that the first build had printed. The
plan read it, on two tries, as outright deletions: try 1 deleted the four grey tool squares and the five sliders
whole; try 2 (below) deleted the printed "4 grey squares" (right) and all five slider names (wrong). n191's "not
this" can only re-point a step at another part; it cannot say "don't do this at all". The only ways out were
Cancel and retype, or Go and fix the fix.

## What was built
- **"skip" on every plan row.** The row's words strike through, its outline leaves the page, and the bar says
  "1 step skipped: ✓ Go leaves it out". Pressing it again ("skipped (undo)") brings the step back.
- **The skip reaches the build.** ✓ Go sends `{action, ids}`; the server drops that step before grounding (found
  by the ids the row showed if the index has shifted), writes `skips.json` in the run record, and the build brief
  gets a "STRUCK FROM THE PLAN" section so the typed fix words are not read as asking for it again
  (`pipeline/src/retarget.ts` `applySkips`, `struckBrief`).
- Hand bench: `session.mjs` op `skip {action}` on a held plan (counts as a pick).
- Tests: `pipeline/tests/n192-skip.test.ts` (5).
