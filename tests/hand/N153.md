# n153: a stroke that starts on a note chip is drawn; a row of cards gets one item each

Found in n150 (round 41 e4 file browser, 58.4 after two fixes).

## What was found
The "small box inside a card ate the card, and its tap painted the page" gap was not about nesting at all.
The note typed on the sidebar ("nav: ...") put its chip right where the first folder card's corner was drawn
next. The pen press landed on the chip, so it **dragged the note** instead of drawing: card 1 was never drawn,
the nav note moved onto the wrong spot, and the yellow icon box drawn next started on the moved chip and was lost
too. Its colour tap then had no box to fill and coloured the whole page (the yellow gradient). Replaying the
same strokes through the overlay showed card 1 and the icon box missing from the marks before any build
(new `marks` and `hit` ops in `session.mjs`).

## What was built
- **A pen stroke that starts on a note chip is a stroke** (`main.ts` `wireNoteDrag`, `beginStroke`): in draw mode a
  press on a chip's words that travels more than 5px hands over to the pen and draws from the press point; the
  note stays put. A plain click still opens the note to edit. Each chip now has a small grip (⋮) at its left: drag
  by the grip to move a note onto another mark (n9). e2e: `tests/e2e/chip-stroke.mjs` (zero tokens).
- **A note with a lead or a look tail spreads over a row of look-alike cards** (`rowwords.ts` `rowListItems`):
  "folders, yellow icon: Client work 14 files, Invoices 32, Photos 208, Archive 9" (a lead before a colon) and
  "Brief.docx 2 MB, ...; blue, green icons" (a look tail after a semicolon) give one item per card in drawn order,
  only when the items are exactly as many as the cards (3 or more), so a sentence with commas never splits by
  chance. Both had been printed whole inside one card. 4 unit tests (`n153-row-list-lead.test.ts`).
