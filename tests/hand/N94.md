# n94: a plain drawn panel is built flat; the bar names every line a note covers

Same strokes, taps and notes as n86 before its fix notes, replayed word for word (replay.mjs). First build only, no fixes.

## Gaps found (from n88)
- **A drawn plain panel came out a floating card** (round 36 c4 florist, n88 try 1): a big box drawn with square
  corners in pale pink and one tap was built with rounded corners and a drop shadow on one try and flat on the next.
- **The bar said "on this line" for a heading note that covers three lines** (florist, and vinyl's two-line heading):
  the thread drawn on the sketch grew to every line, but the words in the bar were worked out without the note's
  own words, so a stacked heading (48-63px pitch, n88) never read as one.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- `inkcolour.ts` `plainPanel` / `notFlat`: a box at least 200x150 filled with one plain colour (no fade, split or
  bands; not a pill, tilted box or outline) and no words on it asking for a card, rounding, a shadow or a lift is told
  as a PLAIN FLAT PANEL (border-radius 0, no box-shadow, no border, one solid colour), and checked on the page: the
  block carrying its colour must have corners of 6px or less, no shadow and no gradient, or the repair is told why.
- Overlay: a note's own words now go with its spot when working out what it covers (`noteMarks`), so the bar says
  "on these 3 lines" the moment a heading note is committed; the bar also updates while typing, and if a new note
  changes what an earlier note covers, the bar names that ("… now on these 2 lines").
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n94-flat-panel.test.ts` (4).
