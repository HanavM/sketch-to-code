# n225: a repeated card keeps its tap, its frame and its captions under; a disc stays centred in its ring

Found in n221 (round 47 c5, tattoo parlor). The note "x4 in a row, dark frames, captions under: black diamond, Diamond $80;
red circle, Red sun $60; black triangle, Peak $90; red bar, Bar $45" on one box, drawn in a dark pen and tapped white, gave
frameless cards with upper-case captions inside, a red first card (fixed only by a note), an upright bar, and a red disc
about 20px off the centre of the ring round it.

## Causes found (read from the build prompts)
- **Four other rules read that one note against it.** One said the text on the page is "x4 in a row, dark frames...".
  One said build 4 rows with made-up sample values. One read "red circle" as a solid red fill of the box: that is the red
  first card. One said the tapped box has no border.
- **Nothing told the build about frames, captions under, or which picture went in which card.**
- **The place check passed the off-centre disc:** it matched the outer ring's element to both drawn rings.
- **The ring check called a real ring wrong** when built as a cream disc with a dark disc over its middle. This one fired in
  tries 1-3, and repairs were spent on it.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A card list:** a note opening with a count, then per card a lower-case picture, a comma and a capitalised caption. Words
  before the colon say how every card looks. Only the captions are printed. Not a table's rows, and never a fill.
- **The tap paints every card, the pen is the frame** when the note asks for frames, unless its words name another
  colour ("white border"). A picture's colour word takes the pen of that colour used in the sketch. A bar is wider than tall.
- **Checked per card:** painted the tapped colour, has a frame, holds a picture, its caption is under it and outside it.
- **A tapped ring in another ring** is told its exact centre (50%, translate) and checked on the disc's own element.
- **A covered disc counts as an outline ring.**
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n225-framed-cards.test.ts` (19). Demo: `out-n225/n225-demo.gif` (target, sketch, before, after).

## How far it reaches
Scanned every kept note that opens with a count or lists items with frames or captions (16 notes): only this one has the
picture-then-caption shape. So this is narrow: the card list fires rarely. The ring-in-a-ring and frame parts are more general.
