# n228: a name and a value typed on or above a long bar go to its two ends

Found in n222 (round 47). "Lisbon, 52%" above a bar printed whole, comma in, percent straight after the name (poll,
19 of its 27 fix words). "On track to save this month, $600" on a band came out centred with the comma left in
(budget). "Total hours, 40 big" on a band made a 40 taller than the band, in the middle, clipped (timesheet, one fix).

## Causes found (from what the build was told)
- **Nothing told or checked the split on a box.** The same form on a row with a drawn line was already split right.
- **Timesheet:** "big" plus a number made the band an *area* (a box that only marks where a big word sits). The first
  try here showed this plainly: the area checks told the repair to take the band's fill off and grow the 40 to 114px.
- **Found on the way:** "x5 rows: Website redo, ...; hours, totals bold" names what fills each row and leaves the
  numbers out on purpose. The listed-rows check (n214) called each made-up hour "not one of the typed rows" and
  a repair stripped them all, so the whole table went empty.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A name and a value** (`endpair.ts`): 1 to 8 words, a comma, a number, price, percent or time with an optional short
  unit, then any look words. A comma inside a number is no break. Lists, counts and notes with other words after the
  comma stay what they were.
- **The bar:** a drawn box at least 240px wide, four times as wide as tall, at most 120px tall, with the note on it or
  up to 44px above it. The widest wins, so a track beats the fill drawn inside it.
- **Where it goes:** on the bar, both inside it, name left, value right, the value no taller than 60% of the bar; above
  the bar, on one line over it, from its left end to its right end. Never a comma, never a stat card, never an area.
- **Checked on the page** (`verifyEndPairs`): both there as their own text, at the two ends, on one line, inside or above
  the bar. A miss goes to repair.
- **Rows that leave their figures out:** when the rows have no values and their look names data (hours, totals, prices,
  amounts, numbers, figures, scores, counts, sums, costs, data), the build is told to make up the numbers and the check
  lets numbers through. A made-up *label* is still caught.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n228-end-pair.test.ts` (6). Demo: `out-n228/n228-demo.gif` (target, before, now).

## Did it help
- **The name and value sat at the two ends of their bar in 5 builds of 6, with no fix note** (poll 2, timesheet 2,
  budget 1). The miss was the timesheet's first try, before the area fix. In timesheet try 2 the pair was right inside

- **Words down on all three pages:** poll 59 to 32, timesheet 51 to 46, budget 92 to 53 (that one is the first build
  only; its donut and rows still need n226 and n227, so the fair number is lower words, not the score).

  unrelated reason. Poll is 79.6 and 73.8 against 78.5: fewer words, about the same or a bit lower score. The budget's

  time. These pages have been seen, so none of this counts toward done; a fresh round has to show it.
