## Gaps found (from n87 and n91)
- "progress bar 82%, grey track to the end; 41 of 50 hours logged" on a purple line: every build printed a big
  "82%" over a thin rule, and dropped the caption.
- The two small pager squares beside the date range, drawn in the dark pen, were built as dark filled buttons
  (a box drawn in a pen colour was always read as filled with it).
- Found this step: the box holding "Weekly target" and the progress note was read as a STAT CARD with value "82%"
  (statcard.ts), and the caption after the semicolon was called a direction (typedcopy.ts). Both told the build to
  print a big 82% and no caption; on try 2 a repair round brought the big 82% back under the stat-card check.
- Still open: try 4 built a taller header and the whole page sat about 13px low (layout 46, the n88 drift gap);
  "Total row, bold" printed "Total row" once (a label word kept).
- Try 3: the build turn wrote the page and then hung for 19 minutes with no end; killed, not scored.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- `progress.ts`: a note naming a progress bar / meter / gauge with a percent (or "N of M") is a bar: told to the
  build as a track the drawn width with that much filled in the line's pen colour, never a printed big percent,
  the typed caption small under it. Checked on the page: a track and a fill about that share of it, no big
  percent text, the caption shown.
- `inkcolour.ts` `inkOutlineBox` + intent2: a box at most 64px a side drawn in dark, nearly neutral ink with no
  tap is an outlined small control (1px light border, no dark fill), told and checked.
- `statcard.ts`: a box with a progress note is never a stat card. `typedcopy.ts`: progress notes are left to
  progress.ts (the caption is copy, not a direction).
