# n155: a side menu typed with no drawn rows stacks from the panel's top, above the button under it

Found in n150/n153 (round 41 e4 file browser: nav ran under the Upload button, "Trash" hidden; e1 bank home: nav
about 130px low).

## What was found
A long list note typed inside a tall side panel was told to the build as copy that "sits where it was typed"
(n21). A person types a long note wherever there is room, so the menu started at the note's spot (file browser
y 128, bank y 235) instead of at the top of the panel, and in the file browser its five rows ran under the
Upload button drawn at y 258.

## What was built
- **Side menus** (`sidelist.ts`, wired in `intent2.ts` and `run2.ts`): a tall box at a page edge (360px+ tall,
  120-420px wide) with a list note typed in its upper 60% and no rows drawn for it. The menu starts under the lowest
  note or mark above it in the panel (+32px), else 24px from the panel's top; only ever moved up. The first drawn box
  under it (its button) caps it: rows are what fits (32-48px), 44px with nothing below. The note's typed spot is moved
  to the start (so no other brief says "at y 130"); the build is told the start, the pitch, and that the button is not
  in the menu's flow (keeps its drawn top and width).
- Checked on the page (`side-menus`): first item near the start, last item above the button, the button at its
  drawn top and width. 5 unit tests (`n155-side-menu.test.ts`). Zero tokens.
