## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A colour tap on a toolbar button pressed the button** (e1: a white tap for the play button landed on the
  bar's "manual" button and switched the overlay into test mode; every stroke after it was lost). The n30 fix
  covers taps on the bar's bare surface only. Worked around by dragging the bar up into empty space.
  Lead: in draw mode a tap on a bar button that sits over drawn ink goes to the ink, or the bar moves itself
  off the drawn area.
- **A fix that removed an invented button was put back** (e5: the pale green box drawn for the first row's
  average cell was built as a made-up "Save Grades" button, pushing the table down 70px. The fix note "no
  button: first row's average cell" was undone by the guard because it "emptied Save Grades"). Lead: when the
  fix note asks for an element to go, emptying it is the fix, not a regression; and a pale box at a table
  column's top is that column's cell.
- **A lone ring at the top of typed rows is still built as an extra mark** (e1: the drawn hollow ring became
  a filled play button above row 1, rows started under it; e4: the grey dot sat on top of row 1's words).
  Same as open n114. The fix notes bound to the built element under the pen, not the drawn ring (e1 added a
  ring to row 1's time; e4 put grey dots at each row's right end).
- **Tabs drawn on a divider were moved below it** (e5: tabs with their underline at the divider were built
  as a band between two dividers; with the invented button, everything below the title drifted ~70px).
- **Rows typed beside a drawn column stop short of it** (e3: rows ran to 660, drawn space to 760; steppers
  only on the row named in the stepper note until a fix).
- **Small ones:** "picked" printed as option text (e2), hollow grey radio rings built solid (e2), middle
  dots dropped from subtitles again (e1, e5), stat label/number order swapped (e4), Total and its value
  stacked instead of one line (e3, fixed by one note).
