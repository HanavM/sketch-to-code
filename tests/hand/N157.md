# n157: a big title is only as big as the gap drawn under it

Found in n156 (round 41 e3 to-do: "Today, huge bold" built at 60px over its "Tuesday, March 10" line, layout 47).

## What was found
"Today, huge bold" was typed on a short level line (115px) and "Tuesday, March 10" on a line drawn 40px under it.
Nothing tied the title's size to that gap, so "huge" was built at 60px and covered a third of the date. The
own-words check saw the overlap after the build, but only as words-on-words, with no size to aim for. A person who
draws two lines 40px apart means a title that fits in 40px; "huge" means the biggest thing here.

## What was built
- **Title sized by the gap under it** (`titlecap.ts`, told in `run2.ts`): a level line under 200px (longer lines are
  sized by their length, line-span), not a divider or part of a drawn stack, whose note asks for big type, with
  another level line 20-100px under it whose words do not ask for big type. Font-size at most 0.95 of the pitch
  (40px gap: 38px). The brief says the words are centred on the line (a line drawn for words marks their middle,
  as everywhere else in the tool), and the subtitle sits centred on its own line, clear under it.
- Checked on the page (`title-caps`): the title's font-size is not more than a fifth over the cap and its letters
  do not run into the words under it; a miss names the size to take it to. 4 unit tests (`n157-title-cap.test.ts`).
  Zero tokens.
