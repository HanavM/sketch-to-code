# n165: a table typed under its drawn header rule; row 1's drawn cells are cells, never buttons

Found in n162 (round 42 e5 gradebook): the sketcher drew the table header's bottom rule (a long line at y 242), and
under it row 1's distinctive cells: a small grey ring at the left (the avatar) and a pale green box at the right
(the tinted Average cell). The columns were typed as one note lower down. The reader called the ring and box "a
toolbar (checkbox + green button)", the build made a "Save Grades" button above the table, and every row came out
about 70px low. The fix note "no button: first row's average cell" removed the button, and the fix guard put it back
because the fix had "emptied Save Grades".

## What was built
- **Table under its rule** (`tablerows.ts`): a note led by "table:" (or grid, columns) naming 2-10 columns, typed in
  open space under a long drawn line (400px or more): that line is the header row's bottom rule and the table's width.
  Marks drawn just under it are row 1: a box hanging from the rule is row 1's cell in the column at its x (the last
  column when it ends at the rule's end), its height the row pitch, its fill that cell's tint; a small ring at the
  left is row 1's leading mark (an avatar), repeated on every row. Told to the build as overriding any reading of
  those marks as a toolbar, checkbox or button, and **checked on the page** (the first column's header sits just
  above the rule, the ring is on every row).
- **"no button" is a removal** (`fixcheck.ts` `dropsKinds`): a fix note asking for a kind of element to go ("no
  button", "not a badge", "remove the banner") is answered by emptying that element, so its words going is not
  "lost copy" and the fix is no longer put back. Other copy is still guarded.
- 6 unit tests (`n165-table-rule.test.ts`, on the real n162 strokes and the real fix note). Zero tokens.
