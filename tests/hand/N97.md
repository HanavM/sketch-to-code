## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A note on a line inside a big filled box binds to the box** (c2): the headline and subtitle notes,
  dropped on their lines inside the rust panel, said "on this box"; I had to drag each onto its line.
  c1's rows note did the same on the stalls card. Lead: inside a box, a note nearest a line goes to the
  line (n68/n72 area).
- **Two headline lines far apart are not one stacked heading** (c2): "Slow clay." with lines 135px apart
  was built as one small line, and the tool **made up copy** ("Thrown slow, glazed by hand.") for the
  second drawn line; a fix asked to remove it. Lead: big line pitch means a big heading over both lines;
  a bare line is never filled with invented words.
- **Invented extras** again: "— Kiln Studio" under the quote (c2), star dots everywhere (c5, asked for a
  few).
- **A box drawn round a big word is built as a black block** (c3 '27): black pen box with no tap became a
  filled black panel with the number in yellow; the fix note made it a yellow block that cut the
  diagonal bands. Lead: area box (n14/n17) must hold on a page with bands, and n78 (dark pen box = outline).
- **A fix note's words get printed** (c3): "one act per line: Fri / Ola..." was pasted into the lineup.
  And the first build split "Fri / Ola Mensah Trio, Sat / ..." at the wrong places (slash read as a break).
- **A ring drawn in a coloured pen is built as a filled disc** (c5), even with "thin ring, no fill" on it
  (the note bound, the build ignored it). The first fix ("only a thin outline, dark inside") bound to the
  hero section instead and turned it into a dim card; a second round undid that and fixed the ring.
  Lead: fix notes on a built shape bind to the shape (n79), and n78 for rings.
- **A colour word in a button note overrides the tap** (c4): "Order a loaf, white bold pill" (white text)
  came out a white button and the tap's dark fill went to the other button.
- **A page tap near the toolbar hit the toolbar** (c4, my slip twice): a tap at the bottom middle switched
  the overlay to Test mode, so nothing I drew registered and the bar said "placed here" for every note.
  Both tries discarded and the page restarted. Lead: the toolbar should step aside for taps (n30 did it
  for colour taps) or a stroke made in Test mode should warn.
- **Nav words built big, as one link or wrapping** (c1, c2, c3 date, c5): each took a fix note.
- **Group notes reach only some marks**: the c3 lineup note bound 2 of 3 lines even when moved.
