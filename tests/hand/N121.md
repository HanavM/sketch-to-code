# n121: a level line drawn in a colour is a bar, and the words beside it stay dark

Found in n120 (round 38 e3, budget, try 1): every category label and amount was built in its bar's colour.

## Why, and what was built
- **Why it happened:** a level line drawn in a coloured pen was told to the build as "that text or rule is coloured X"
  and checked as *text* of that colour within 40px of the line. When the build drew bars with dark labels the check
  failed and the repair was told "paint its text exactly #f97316", overruling the prompt's own "labels stay dark" rule.
- **Bars are read as bars** (`barlabels.ts`): a level line at least 120px long, drawn in a pen that is not dark, with no
  words typed on it and not a scribbled text line, is a bar. A vivid line is a bar on its own; a pale or grey line (a
  light track, a grey bar) only beside a vivid bar in the same block, so a white heading line on a dark page stays text.
- **Told as a bar:** a thin filled strip about 6-10px tall, about the drawn length, in that colour; its label and amount
  in the block's normal dark text, never the bar colour.
- **Checked as a bar:** a thin block painting that colour on the drawn line (within 18px), instead of text of that colour.
- **Labels checked** (new `bar-labels` check): words just above, on or at the ends of a drawn bar must not take any
  bar's colour (ΔE 20), unless a note named a colour for them.
