# n226: colours tapped round a ring are its slices

Found in n222 (round 47 e5, budget donut). Four colours tapped round a ring came out a square fade in the preview and in every first
build, and in one build lost the hole. Only a fix note got a real donut. n227 fixed the rows beside it and left this.

## Causes found
- **Taps on one mark could only mean a fade.** Three or more colours round a ring went down the same path as two taps on a banner.
- **The chart's shares had to be typed even though the rows beside it already held the amounts.** That was 13 of the page's 53 words.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Three or more different colours spread round a round mark are flat slices.** Worked out from the ink. Taps down one side, in a
  line through the middle, or on a square stay what they were. Two colours stay a fade. A note saying fade, gradient or glow keeps a fade.
- **Each slice runs half way to its neighbours' taps,** with the nearest boundary turned to 12 o'clock when it is within 30 degrees.
- **A round mark drawn inside on the same centre is the hole.**
- **Typed shares win:** "40, 25, 20, 15 percent" go clockwise from the top. **With none typed, a note holding one amount per slice
  gives the shares,** in the order the colours were tapped, the same order the rows' colour marks already use.
- **The preview shows the slices and the hole straight away.** It works from the ink only, so it shows the drawn split, not typed shares.
- **Told and checked on the page:** round, at the drawn size and place, hard edges, right colours in order, each slice within 10 degrees
  of its share, hole present. A fade, a blend or a lost hole fails the check. An SVG chart passes on its colours only; its shares are not checked.
- The page snapshot keeps more of a conic background so the check can read every stop.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n226-slices.test.ts` (10), plus one in n227's. Demo: `out-n226/n226-demo.gif` (target, sketch, before, now).

## How far it reaches
Scanned every kept hand sketch: **one page has a mark with three or more colours tapped on it, this one**, and it now reads as slices. So no
kept sketch changes by mistake, but this is narrow so far. Fresh pages will show how often it gets used.

## Two false checks found on the way, both fixed
The first three builds each ran a repair round that stalled 90 to 152 s with nothing edited. Reading their records:
- **The row check took the "$600" in the chart's hole for the Food row's "$600"** (a bug from n227), in all three. Now one copy in the row's own box is enough, and it still fails if the row has none.
- **The colour check could not see a colour inside a chart's stops or on an SVG shape,** in 2 of the 3. It now reads both.

With both gone the last build passed every check first time: **105 s build, 188 s total, 40.5k billed, against 382 to 529 s and 53k to 61k.** That is one build.
