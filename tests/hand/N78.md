# n78: a box drawn in a light grey pen (no tap) is an outline, not a grey block

Found in round 38 (n108): on three light app pages, every box drawn with a light grey pen to show a faint card
frame (room cards, calendar, search field, Email / Call buttons) was built as a solid grey block. A person reaches
for a light grey pen to draw the thin frame they see, and taps when they want a fill.

## Built
- A box (or pill) drawn in a nearly neutral light grey pen (#9ca3af to #e5e7eb range; not the palest greys such as
  #f3f4f6, which stay a fill, and never a coloured pen) with no colour tapped inside is read as an OUTLINE.
- The build is told: a block exactly there with a thin 1px border in that grey, white or the surface under it
  inside, never filled grey; its content sits inside the frame.
- The built page is checked: the block there must have a frame (border, outline or ring shadow) and must not be
  filled with the pen's grey; a miss goes to the repair round. Zero tokens.
- Not built yet: a "no fill" fix note keeping the border (second half of the node); left as a follow-up.
