# n112: a part drawn once in the first of a set is on every member; ticked options are checkboxes

Found in round 38 (n108, still wrong after n111):
- e4 job board: five like job cards drawn one under another, a small green box typed "Apply" drawn in the FIRST
  card only. Every build put Apply on card 1 alone.
- e2 contacts: a list panel typed with ten "name company" rows and one small ring at its top left (the first
  row's avatar). The build made it a search icon; no row had an avatar.
- e4: the filter note with ticks ("Full time ✓, Part time, Remote ✓; Level: ...") came out as checkboxes in one
  build and plain lines in another, centred in the panel.

## Built
- **Part drawn once in a set** (`drawnonce.ts`): three or more like-sized boxes lined up (column or row, none
  inside another, even pitch). A small box or ring inside the first member, with nothing like it at the same spot
  in any other, is a part every member has. Codegen is told to build it in each member at the same offset, same
  size and label; the page is checked for it at each member's spot (with its label when typed). Not when its
  note says it is one of a kind (only, featured, popular, selected, current...), and not when drawn in a middle member.
- **First row's leading part**: a big box with a typed list of 3+ rows (split by ";") and a lone small ring or
  box with no words at its top left is every row's leading part (avatar, icon). Told and checked (a column of
  like-sized marks down the box). Lists that are checkbox notes are left out.
- **Checkbox lists** (`checklist.ts`): a note with 3+ comma options, at least one ticked (✓, ✔, [x]), is a
  checkbox list: "Name:" before a group is its heading, ticked options checked, one per line from the panel's
  top. Checked: every option its own piece of text, no ✓ printed. Zero tokens.
