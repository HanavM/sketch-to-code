# n141: a white or grey pen box with no tap on a dark page is an outline; a strong pen card noted picked / pale fill is a pale card

Found in n138 (round 40 c4) and n139 (round 40 e1, e5); the same miss shows in round 38 c3 (film fest "See the programme").

## What was built
- **Dark-page frames** (`inkcolour.ts` `inkDarkOutline`, wired in `intent2.ts` and `penFrameBox`): a box drawn in a white or
  grey pen with no tap, on a dark surface (luminance under 70), clearly lighter than it, holding no other drawn box, and with no
  note asking for a fill, is an OUTLINE: a thin border in the pen colour, background transparent so the dark shows through.
  The dark-page twin of n78 (grey) and n131 (light pens on light pages). Told in the brief and checked on the page by the
  existing frame check (no pen-colour fill, a border present).
- Kept as fills, on purpose: a box that holds other drawn boxes (round 39 e1's white search bar round three inputs on a blue
  hero), coloured pens (a red pass button, a mint card), and dark tiles close to the page (round 38 e5's #3f3f46 video tiles).
- **Picked cards** (`selectedChip`): the n135 selected-chip rule now covers cards up to 480x420, and "pale fill" / "light
  tint" / "soft background" counts as asking for the pale look, not a solid block. Round 40 e5: "Team picked, pale fill" on a
  purple pen card came out solid purple; now pale lilac with a purple border.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 14 unit tests (`packages/pipeline/tests/n141-dark-outline.test.ts`).
