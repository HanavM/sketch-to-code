# n193: "place it" gives a step with no target its place by a tap

Found replaying round 44 e3 photo editor in n192: the fix note's "sky fades to pale orange" was read as a DESIGN
step with no target, so n191's "not this" had nothing to re-point and the sky was painted into the crop frame.

## What was built
- **"place it" on every plan row that points at nothing.** The row says "no part picked: the build decides where";
  "place it" then a tap on the page puts that part on the row (outlined on the page, "placed ✓ (again?)"), and the
  same-spot re-tap climb from n191 works here too.
- **The place reaches the build.** The client sends `{action, from: null, srcLoc, rect, instruction}`. For an add or
  design step the server makes the tapped part its container and the brief says "do it in and on that part — if
  it is a look (a colour, a fade, a sky) it is that part's own look — and in no other element (not the one it
  was read into)". For any other kind the part becomes its target. A target-less step is found by its words if
  the plan's index has shifted (`pipeline/src/retarget.ts` `applyRetargets`).
- Skip ignores a placed part's id. Hand bench: `pick {action, from: "new", x, y}`.
- Tests: `pipeline/tests/n193-place.test.ts` (4).
