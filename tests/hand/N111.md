# n111: typed copy with dots or a number stays as typed

Found in round 38 (n108, still wrong after n78):
- e1 hotel: the dark bar typed "Reserve · 4 nights" was built as a stat card, a big "4" over a small "Reserve".
- e4 job board: "Product designer, Fernway · Remote · $95k" became the title "Product designer, Fernway", a lone
  "Remote" under it and "$95k" moved to the card's right end; the hotel rooms the same ("Garden double, Queen bed").

## Built
- **Button labels** (`buttonlabel.ts`): a short note that starts with an action word (Reserve, Book, Pay, Apply,
  Continue, Get...) and holds a number or a dot is a button's label: never a stat card, never split into cells.
  The build is told to print it exactly as typed on one line in its box; the page is checked for it as one piece
  of text. Navs ("Shop · Visit · Recipes", three dotted parts) and nouns (Order #48213, OPEN WED - SUN) stay out.
- **Name, comma, dotted details line** (`rowdata.ts commaDotCells`): "Title, a · b · c[, $value]" is the name,
  ONE details line kept as typed with its dots, and a side value. Told to the build and checked on the page (the
  details line must be one text item). Navs and words placed round a mark ("nav right ROUTES · CREW") and a
  time at the end ("to me · 9:14 AM") keep the old cells.
- Scan of the 888 notes typed in all hand rounds: 4 notes now read as button labels (Reserve · 4 nights,
  Continue · $30.50, Pay 2.60, Get Prism 2), 8 as name + details line (the round 38 rooms and jobs). Zero tokens.
