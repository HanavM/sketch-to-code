## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **No hexagon** (c1): eight 6-corner strokes were read as circles and built as ovals, twice. Lead: a
  closed stroke with 5-8 clear corners is a polygon of that many sides, built as that shape (clip-path).
- **Coloured-pen outlines lose their colour** (c1): eight hexagons each drawn in its own pen colour, no
  taps, came out solid black on the first build; a fix note restored the colours. Lead: a closed shape
  drawn in a coloured pen with a "solid" note is filled in its pen colour.
- **A group note over a cluster reaches 1-2 marks** (c1 hexagons, c4 four ruled rows): the note binds to
  the nearest shape(s), not the whole group around it.
- **A whole drawn shape was dropped** (c2): the sun ring (yellow and pink taps, stripes across) was
  missing from the first build entirely; a fix note brought it back as two flat halves, not a fade.
- **A coloured-pen box with no tap became a solid fill** (c2 grid area pink, c4 dark box round the
  heading became a black block with white words). "Ground stays dark" did not undo the pink fill.
  Lead: n78 again: a pen box with no tap is an outline or an area, never a fill.
- **"outline only" ignored** on the first build (c2 button filled yellow); a fix made it right.
- **Ruled row lines read as text baselines** (c4): each line I drew got a row on it, so rows 1-2 crowded
  together and dividers vanished; the fix added thin dividers but kept the crowding. Lead: in a ruled
  list, rows sit between drawn lines.
- **Typed punctuation dropped**: "·" in "FRI 13 NOV · 8PM" and in the nav (c2, c4).
- **Text placed up from its drawn line** (c1): heading and subtitle built ~70-100 px above their lines.
- **A tap under the toolbar** (c3) did not fill the front wave; a second tap elsewhere did.
