# n33: a spotlight from taps; a centred quote keeps its drawn size and lines

Gaps from round 31 c5 (spotlight quote, n27/n32): the page is a purple glow in the middle of a near-black
page, and the only way to say it was two page taps, which built a straight top-to-bottom fade. The two long
centred lines drawn 60px apart for the quote were not read as display copy (the heading rule starts at 64px),
so the quote came out as ~20px body text.

## What was built
- **A spotlight from taps.** One page tap with three or more page taps round it in another colour (they
  surround it, no gap wider than a half turn) is a centre glow: the centre tap's colour at that spot, fading
  out to the ring's colour by the ring's distance. Shown on the sketch the moment the pen lifts (radial fill
  under the ink, and the status says "spotlight"), told to the build as an exact radial-gradient, and checked
  on the page: a linear fade with the same colours now fails and goes to repair.
- **A centred quote is display copy.** Two or three long lines centred on each other (not sharing a left
  edge), 48-63px apart, are one text block sized from the pitch (about pitch / 1.3); left-aligned lines that
  close stay list rows.
- **Stacked text keeps its drawn lines (new page check).** Every text block the ink sized is found on the
  page; it fails when it is a line or more taller than drawn (it wrapped into more lines) or its letters are
  far off the drawn size. The repair is told to drop forced line breaks and wrap in the drawn width; the build
  brief now says to break lines with the width, not a forced break.
- Bench: the app server's own output can be kept in a file (`S2C_APP_LOG`), so a run that dies says why.
