# n123: a big drawn panel built far below its drawn top is moved there in code, no model round

Found in n122 try 3 (round 38 e3, budget): the Categories panel was built 124px under its drawn top. The big-panel check
named it with the exact pixels, and the repair turn thought for 151 s, made no edit and was stopped; likeness 30.6
against 62.3 for the two runs that built the panel in place.

## What was built
- **Direct panel lift** (`panellift.ts`, called at the top of every verify round in `run2.ts`): before anything is
  judged, every big drawn panel (160px+ tall, 200px+ wide, not content of a bigger drawn panel) is matched to its built
  element by drawn x and width. When it sits more than 32px below its drawn top, the tool writes
  `position: relative; top: -<px>` on that element's own tag (merged into its inline style), waits for the page to
  reload and snapshots again. Zero tokens, a few hundred milliseconds.
- **Only when it is safe**: a relative offset moves the panel and what is inside it and nothing else reflows, but it
  could slide the panel over something above it (why repairs are told never to use a negative top). So it refuses
  unless the strip it moves into, across its width, holds only its own columns and page; it also refuses a component
  tag, an element rendered more than once, one already positioned or offset (style or classes), a spread or non-plain
  style, and a panel built too high. Each refusal is written to the run's stages with its reason, and the big-panel
  check then goes to the repair turn as before.
- The run record (`result.json`) lists every lift made (`panelLifts`).
