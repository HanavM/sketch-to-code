# n113: a note that says what a block is gives its copy and look

Found in round 38 (n108, still wrong after n111): e1 hotel booking. A tall box typed "November 2026 calendar,
12 and 16 magenta circles, 13–15 pale pink". Every build printed the whole note as the box's heading and laid the
month out Sunday-first with the marks on the wrong days.

## Built
- **Calendar notes** (`calendar.ts`): a note naming a month, a year and "calendar" describes the block. The
  build is told: heading is the month and year only; weekday letters; the real month (days in it, day 1 under its
  true weekday, computed); weeks start Monday (ISO) unless the note says Sunday ("Sunday first", "S M T W T F S");
  each "<days> <colour> circles" part is a filled circle behind those days, each "<range> <colour>" a soft fill.
  Never print the note, "calendar" or the colour words. Checked on the page: heading present and the note not
  printed; every day 1..N present as a number; days clustered into 7 columns, each day in its weekday column.
- **Typed copy** no longer tells the build that the calendar note is copy (that line is what printed it).
- **Counts** (`counts.ts`): found on the first replay. "16 magenta circles" was read as "exactly 16 circles", and
  "4 nights" as 4 things; the repair rounds then rewrote the calendar to show 16 circled days and hid 10 days.
  Calendar notes and button labels now claim no counts. Zero tokens.
