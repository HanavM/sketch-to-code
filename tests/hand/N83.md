# n83: text typed white on a dark page never vanishes; one note on a row of pills gives each pill its word

Round 35 e3 (workout log), n82 first build: "Push day, big bold white" came out #121418 on the #121418 page
(invisible), and the pill row printed "Week Month Year, dark pills" on the first pill.

## Why
- The page was tapped near-black, and the pen kept that colour for the next strokes. The line drawn under
  "Push day" was read as "this text is #121418" (ink colour overrules typed words), so the title was built in
  the page colour. The readability check skipped it because its colour was typed ("white").
- The note sat on the first of three look-alike pills; ", dark pills" was not read as a style tail ("pills" is
  not a look word), so the first pill's own words were the whole note.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- A mark drawn in the page's own colour is never painted that colour: a pen colour within a hair of the tapped
  page is dropped from the mark (taps are kept); the typed words or the default decide (`dropPageInk`).
- The readability check flags text in the very colour behind it (contrast under 1.35) even when its colour was
  typed.
- One note on a row of look-alike pills or tabs whose words are as many as the pills (three or more, or two when
  another pill's note confirms its word) gives each pill its word in drawn order (`splitRowNotes`); words that name
  the mark ("dark pills", "rounded tabs") are a style tail, never copy.
