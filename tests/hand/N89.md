# n89: the drawn first row is the template; "5 rows like this" needs no typed rows

Round 36 (n87): on transactions and email the first row was drawn as one line (square, name, a middle column
far right, an amount at the end, about 56px tall) with "5 rows like this" / "7 rows like this" plus every row
typed out (131 and 119 words). Both first builds stacked the middle column under the name and made rows
78-115px tall; a fix note restored the drawn row both times.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- `rowtemplate.ts`: a note asking for N rows like this finds the drawn first row inside its mark (the topmost
  level band of 2+ strokes spanning a good part of the mark; a lone heading line is not the row). Its parts are
  told left to right with their drawn x (a square or dot is an icon, a line is a text column, a line ending near
  the right edge is right aligned), and every row's pitch is the drawn row's height (tallest part + padding, or
  twice the gap to a drawn divider). Checked on the page: each drawn column holds text on the row's line, and
  rows step at about the drawn pitch.
- A bare "5 rows like this, thin dividers" (nothing typed for the rows) now asks for SAMPLE rows that fit the
  page; the sample words are allowed. Before, the look tail "thin dividers" was read as a typed row and printed
  as the first row's name (`rowdata.ts`).
- Found on try 1: "stat cards: small grey label, big number under" bound to the stat card and the list card,
  and both were built as solid grey blocks. A small, muted or number-side "label" is words, not a grey block
  (`solids.ts`).
