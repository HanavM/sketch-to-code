## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **The page drifts down from a tall header** (c4, c5): nav drawn at y 44 built at y 55 with a big
  header; c5's whole hero and the sea bands slid ~200px down (layout 20). The fix note ("too low: sits
  on the drawn lines") lifted everything but **deleted the header** (logo and nav gone). Lead: hold the
  header to its drawn lines (n24/n25 did this for settings only) and a repair that lifts content must
  never remove blocks.
- **A heading of 2-3 drawn lines binds its note to the first line only** (c1, c4; known from round 35
  c2): c1's heading was one cut-off line until a fix ("two lines, wraps after back"); c4's three lines
  bound "on this line" and the heading came out right-aligned and low. Lead: a note on a stack of level
  lines with nothing between them covers the stack (n11 did this for big headlines only).
- **A wobbly hand circle is built wobbly** (c1): the record came out as a lumpy blob traced from the
  stroke; the fix ("a true circle") made it round but moved and shrank it off its drawn spot. Lead: a
  closed stroke near a circle snaps to a circle at the drawn centre and size (it does for small rings).
- **A box drawn in black pen with no tap is built filled** (c3, known n78): "See lessons" came out a
  black button; one fix made it an outline.
- **Pen lines drawn as text places are pasted as white rules** (c3): the three stat lines became white
  underlines, and the row note's "stats:" was printed. One fix split each stat into number and label,
  but put "Tue" as the label and "7 to 11 pm" as the number.
- **A fix note on a nav row changes one word** (c2): "small regular, spaced out" bound to "Rooms" only.
- **A tap meant for the page that lands inside a shape fades that shape** (c2, my slip): the pale tap
  sat inside the back mountain and made it a sideways fade; a fix put it right.
- **SALE built smaller than its drawn line** (c1): line drawn 525px long, word built ~370px wide.
- **No way to say "repeat"** (c3, c5): the 6x6 checkerboard and the four sea bands needed a note or
  four boxes; known from round 35.
- **Things not asked for**: rounded corners and a drop shadow on the chessboard and the stats band (c3),
  a faint diagonal fade on the florist's pink panel (c4).
