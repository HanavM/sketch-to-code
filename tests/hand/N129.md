# n129: a ring noted as an arc, track or progress is built hollow, never a solid disc

Found in n127 (round 39 e2 smart home, e3 focus timer, 2 of 2): a ring drawn in a coloured pen with no tap was told
"the ring is filled with <pen colour>", so "orange arc over grey track" and "red arc 3/4 on pink track" both came out
as a big solid disc; a 2-word fix "hollow ring" was needed on each.

## What was built
- **Ring words make a ring a line** (`hollowring.ts`, `intent2.ts`): a ring (60px or more) drawn in a coloured pen, no
  tap, whose own notes (bound to it, or typed inside it) say arc, track, progress, gauge, dial, ring, donut, meter or
  hollow (and not solid, filled, disc, dot, badge, avatar, sun...) is flagged hollow instead of filled. Its thickness
  comes from its size (about 6% of the diameter, 8-28px), and a typed share (3/4, 75%, "half") and track colour
  ("grey track") are read from the note.
- **Told as a hollow ring** (`inkcolour.ts`): outer edge at the drawn box, the arc in the pen colour clockwise from 12
  o'clock for the typed share, a pale track ring under it, the inside the surface colour, words in the hole; built as
  SVG circles (fill none) or a conic disc with an inner disc on top.
- **Checked on the page**: a disc about the drawn size, centred on the ring, painting the pen colour with nothing
  covering its middle fails (with a one-line repair); an SVG ring or a disc with a hole passes.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 3 unit tests on the round 39 notes and geometry.
