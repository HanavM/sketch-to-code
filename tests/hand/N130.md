# n130: a list note on a row drawn short fills the whole row; state and per-row words are never printed

Found in n127 (round 39 e4 vet booking): the person drew two of the five day boxes (Tue filled, Wed empty) and typed
"Mon 14, Tue 15, Wed 16, Thu 17, Fri 18" on the Wed box. The build made Wed "14 / Wed 16" (the note's first number
in the box it was typed on) and a one-word fix "Wed 16" only made it worse. "Biscuit selected, avatar" was printed in
the pet pill, and "12 time slots 09:00-16:30, 4 a row" was printed as a caption.

## What was built
- **A comma list of items of one form is a row's items** (`rowwords.ts`): 3-8 items, same word count, a digit in all
  or none, no look words ("Mon 14, Tue 15, ..."). When fewer boxes are drawn than items, the drawn boxes are told
  which items they are from where they sit after the row's start (the left edge of the heading and boxes above:
  Tue box = item 2, Wed = item 3), and the build is told the whole row: every item one box, the drawn size and
  pitch, the first at the row's start, each box only its own words.
- **Checked on the page**: every item is in the row's band, no text piece holds two items, and a drawn box never
  shows words of another item ("14" over "Wed 16" fails, with a one-line repair).
- **Never a stat card** (`statcard.ts`): such a list is no longer read as "value 14, label Mon" (that is what put the
  big 14 in the Wed box in the first live try).
- **Directions kept off the page** (`typedcopy.ts`): a comma tail naming the item's state or kind (", avatar",
  ", selected", ", faded") or how many a row (", 4 a row", ", 3 per row") is a direction; a state word ending a
  short label ("Biscuit selected") is a direction; the look-alike pills keep only "Biscuit" as their words.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 9 unit tests.
