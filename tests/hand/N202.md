# n202: a listing card is never painted with a giant word from its notes

Found in n198 (round 45 e1 car rental): the first build painted a huge grey "500" across the Fiat card, and the
fix spent on it went wrong (n200 fixed the fix). This step stops the build painting it at all.

## Why it happened
The card was drawn as a box round two marks of its own (a picture box and a Select button) with two notes on it:
"Fiat 500 bold, 4 seats · Automatic" and "$38 big bold, per day". A box needed 3 marks inside to count as a card,
so with 2 it was read as a big word's *area*: "$38 big bold" made it one, and the word was then taken from the
*first* note ("Fiat 500" → "500"). The build was told to fill the card with "500", and the check passed it
("1 big word filling its box").

## What was built
- **A box holding a drawn mark and carrying two or more notes is a card of several parts, never one big word's
  area** (`areas.ts`). A lone big-word box ("huge outlined 09") is still an area.
- **The big word comes from the note that asks for size**, never from a plain copy note beside it.
- **A blown-up echo is caught on the page** (`ownwords.ts`): text that is part of a typed line, copied out at twice
  its size or more and painted over it ("500" over "Fiat 500"), fails the words check with "take the big copy out".
- Tests: `pipeline/tests/n202-no-giant-echo.test.ts` (5).
- Wide check (151 hand-sketched pages, boxes approximated from strokes and note spots): of 23 big-word area boxes,
  only 2 change: car rental, and r41 bank home's balance card ("$4,286.50 huge bold", "Total balance", "Main
  account"), which n156 had already noted as wrongly read as an area.

## Did it help
- Yes: car rental now lands 85 with **no fix at all** (was 78.7 with two fixes, 85.3 with one), 16 fewer words,

  total time includes a stretch where the replay client was cut off and `done` was called by hand.
