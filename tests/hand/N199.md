# n199: a pen line drawn for a heading is where its words go and their colour, never a bar

Found in n197 (round 45 c3, pizza oven): the headline was drawn as two red level lines with "Fired at 900 / degrees,
huge bold" typed between them. Every build painted a red bar over and under the headline and made the words dark; a
fix note turned the words red but the bars only changed colour (79.8 with one fix, 59 words).

## Why it happened
- A coloured level line with no words of its own is read as a bar (n121, for budget bars). The heading note was bound
  to both lines together, so neither line had words of its own.
- The n136 guard (a sentence typed on coloured lines makes them its lines) only counts five-plus plain words: this
  note has a number, a slash and two look words, and its second line is 42px from the note (the guard reached 40px,
  and spread only 48px between lines; these heading lines are 82px apart).
- So the brief said "a BAR ... not text" and the ink check asked for a red strip on each line: the tool itself asked
  for the bars.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **A heading note makes its lines words** (`headingNote` in `barlabels.ts`): copy with a real word, at most one number,
  no money or bar words, and a big look (huge, heading, title ...) or lines split with " / ". It reaches 64px from the
  note and spreads to lines stacked up to 120px apart at the same start. Those lines are never bars.
- **Told plainly**: "those WORDS are coloured #d2462e and sit on that line; the pen line itself is never painted: no
  bar, strip, rule, border or underline".
- **Checked on the page**: the lines-not-rules check now covers these lines too (their note was bound to the whole
  stack), and counts any empty strip up to about 10px tall along the line as a bar (was 4.5px).
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n199-heading-lines.test.ts` (4).

## Did it help
- **Yes.** The headline comes out red on its drawn lines with no bars on the first build, both tries, so the fix note
  is no longer needed: +4.4 to +5.0 points with 10 fewer words, about 4.5 minutes faster and fewer tokens.
- Not done in this step (split out as its own node): the bike page's untapped black rings built as clipped discs; the
  two-factor page's short white line under a heading pasted as a rule (a white pen is never a bar, so that is a
  different path).
