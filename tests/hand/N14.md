# n14: a box round a big word is no card; a slanted line across the page is the colour edge (goal 2026-10-27)

What was built (branch try/n2-shape-vocab, on top of n11's try/n4-look-fix):

- **A box round a big word or number is that word's area.** When the note typed
  on a box says a big word or number ("huge outlined 09, white") and names no
  thing (card, panel, button, photo...), the box is flagged `area`. It stays a
  plain shape mark even when the reading calls the number a depiction: before,
  the box outline became a drawn path, and the drawing check asked for that
  closed form to be filled, which is how the black card behind the 09 came
  about. Codegen is told on the mark: transparent, no fill, border or shadow.
  The page is checked: nothing built over that box may paint a fill, border or
  shadow of its own (snapshot now records border and shadow).
- **A slanted line across most of the page is the edge between two colours.**
  Flagged `edge`; codegen gets the exact clip-path polygon that puts the
  boundary on the drawn line, and the page is checked for a clipped layer whose
  slanted side lies on it (snapshot now records clip-path).
