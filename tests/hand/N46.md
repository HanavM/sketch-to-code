# n46: a row of words typed on one drawn line stays within that line

## What was built
- **Said before the build:** words typed on a centred level line are now told, besides "centre them on x ...", that if
  they are a row of separate items (nav links round a logo, tags, a menu) the whole row stays within the drawn line's
  x span as one centred group with even gaps, never spread to the page edges (no space-between, no ml-auto)
  (packages/pipeline/src/textplace.ts via `rowBrief`).
- **Checked after the build:** `row-span` (packages/pipeline/src/rowspan.ts, wired into the build's checks). For each
  level line with 3+ copy words, the separate pieces of text near the line made only of those words are found; if
  the row runs more than 12% (min 40px) past either end of the drawn line, the repair is told the span, the order
  and a gap. A single heading is left to the heading checks. Zero tokens.
- Unit tests: packages/pipeline/tests/rowspan.test.ts (5).
