# n122: a ring drawn in coloured arcs is checked as the chart; bar rows keep their drawn pitch

Found in n121 (round 38 e3, budget): the ink-colour check failed every run on four "filled outlines" (one per arc of
the donut), and in try 2 the bar rows sat 20-30px below their drawn lines while repairs were only told "build a bar".

## Why, and what was built
- **Ring arcs left to the chart** (`drawnring.ts` `ringStrokeIds`, `inkcolour.ts`): the ink pass read each coloured arc
  of a drawn ring as its own filled blob and told and checked a block the size of each arc. Those arcs are now skipped
  by the ink-colour brief and check; the ring is told and checked once, as a chart (n118's drawn-rings check).
- **Bars stacked in a column are rows at the drawn pitch** (`barlabels.ts` `barPitchBrief`): three or more bars drawn
  one under another are told as rows of one list, with each bar's drawn y and the pitch (budget: 70px), first row where
  drawn.
- **A bar built off its line is named with its offset** (`barBuilt` `offBy`, `barRowsHint`): when no bar is on the line
  but one of that colour sits within 70px, the repair is told how far off it is and why: the whole list starts low by
  the same amount (take that out above the first row), or the rows repeat at another pitch (make each row the drawn
  pitch). Before, it was told "build a thin strip there", which made no change.
- **Content inside a big panel is not a panel of its own** (`drift.ts`): tries 1-2 failed the big-panel check with
  "top at 110px, drawn at 230px": the region of the bar rows (drawn inside Categories) was matched by size to the panel
  round it. A region inside a bigger drawn region (not a page-wide backdrop) is now left to that panel.
