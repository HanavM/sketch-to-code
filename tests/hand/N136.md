# n136: lines a sentence was typed on are where its words go, never coloured bars

Found in n126 / n128 (round 39 c1 honey co.): the subtitle was drawn as two level lines in a brown pen with its
sentence typed between them. Since n121 a wordless level line in a non-dark pen is a bar, and the note was not "on"
either line, so both lines were read as bars: every build drew two brown strips under the subtitle (2 of 2 in n128).

## What was built
- **Sentence notes** (`barlabels.ts` `proseNote`): five or more words, nearly all words (at most one number, no money
  or %), not a `;` list, and not about bars, tracks, progress, meters, sliders or charts.
- **Copy lines** (`proseLineIds`): a sentence note whose point is over a level line's span and within 40px of it (on
  it, or between two lines of a paragraph) marks that line, and every line stacked with it (same start within 16px,
  rows no more than 48px apart), as copy. Its ink colour is the words' colour (`copy: true`), never a bar.
- Bars stay bars: the budget page's list note (labels with amounts) is not a sentence; checked in tests.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 5 unit tests.
