# n134: a light pen box with no tap stays an outline even when its group is read as a picture

Found in n133 (round 39 e4 vet booking): the light-mint "Luna" pill, outlined 2 of 2 in n131, came out a filled
mint block in both n133 builds. Cause: the reading took the pet picker ("Biscuit selected, avatar") as a drawn
picture, so its boxes were traced as paths instead of read as boxes, and n131's light-pen outline rule (which only
looked at boxes) never ran; the traced shape was then filled in its pen colour. It depended on how the model grouped
the marks, so it came and went between builds of the same sketch.

## What was built
- **Pen frames are never pictures** (`inkcolour.ts` `penFrameBox`, used in `intent2.ts`): a straight box drawn in a
  light pen (a light grey, n78, or any light hue on a light surface, n131) with no tap and no fill note is kept as a
  box whatever the reading calls its group, so the outline rule and its page check always apply.
- Zero tokens, no model call. 2 unit tests.
