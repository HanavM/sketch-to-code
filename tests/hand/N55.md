# n55: a note typed just above a paragraph is about the paragraph, not the heading over it

Round 33 (n49) had to drag a note on two pages (c1 surf, c4 herb farm): the paragraph's note, typed just
above its first line, was also within reach of the heading's last line, so it took the heading too. On surf
the same note also broke the heading in two: the heading's second line "had its own sentence" (the
paragraph's words), so the heading was built as one small line (n51 replay: layout 53).

## What was built
- **A note between two text lines goes to the nearer one when the other is another block**
  (`packages/ink/src/notefocus.ts` `dropFarSideLines`): the note is clearly nearer one line (the other at
  least twice as far and 10px further), the two lines sit on opposite sides of it, and the far line is a
  block gap away (more than a paragraph's wrap pitch) or has a note of its own closer to it. A note between
  two wraps of one paragraph still takes both. Same rule in the overlay's thread and in the build.
- **A note belongs to the line it is nearest when headings are read** (`packages/ink/src/textmark.ts`): a
  note only counts as "on" a line (its own sentence, or the line is not bare) when that line is its nearest
  (ties within 6px count for both). The paragraph note no longer splits the two-line heading above it.
- Also fixes the round 33 c4 labels: "Bright and zesty" typed under "Lemon thyme" no longer takes the
  label line too ("on this line", was "on these 2 lines").
