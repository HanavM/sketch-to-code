# n15: a coloured badge or panel is that plain shape, solid; bare rows are no card (goal 2026-10-27)

What was built (branch try/n2-shape-vocab, on top of n14):

- **A ring or box typed with a colour and a plain thing is that thing, solid.**
  "lime badge FREE COFFEE AFTER" on a ring was read as a picture of a badge:
  its outline became a path and it came out a faint brown disc (n14, both
  tries). Now a ring or box whose own note names a colour word and a plain
  thing (badge, pill, chip, tag, button, panel, card, tile...) is flagged
  `solid`: it stays a clean shape even when the reading calls it a depiction,
  codegen is told "one element exactly there, circle or rectangle, a clear
  opaque lime", and the page is checked: an element over the mark must paint
  that colour family, opaque and unblurred (snapshot now records opacity and
  filter). Glass, frosted, glow and blurred notes are never solid.
- **A box typed only with rows is where the rows sit, not a card.**
  "3 rows: Route, Start, Groups" on a box came out a white rounded card with a
  shadow (n9-n14); the target's rows sit bare on the page. A box whose note only
  lists rows (no card, panel or colour named) is an area: told no fill, border,
  shadow or rounded panel, and checked like an area box.
