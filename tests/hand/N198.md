## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A fix note dropped on a stray element deletes its whole container** (e1, worst gap): the first build
  painted a huge grey "500" behind the Fiat card (the word from "Fiat 500" blown up as a decoration). A note
  on it, "delete the huge grey 500", bound to the card's div and deleted the whole first card; a second fix
  brought it back with invented words ("Book", "Auto", "/day"). Lead: a delete fix removes only the smallest
  element under the note, and the plan names what it will delete before Go.
- **A big word from a note painted as a giant background decoration** (e1 "500"): same family as earlier
  giant-word gaps.
- **Digits typed for boxes replace the boxes** (e2): "4, 8, 1 big bold in first three boxes" built three
  huge bare digits and dropped the three drawn boxes; a fix put them back.
- **A count on a drawn strip crams copies inside it** (e3): "6 strips: Drums, Bass, ..." on one drawn strip
  built five tiny strips inside the first; a fix laid them out but not at the drawn strip's width or panel.
  Lead: "N like this" repeats the drawn mark at its size across the open space beside it.
- **Untapped outline ring built as a filled disc** (e3 knobs; same as r45 c4 wheels).
- **Second line of a stacked headline got invented copy** (e2 "Manage your security settings."): the note
  bound to the first lavender line only, so the second line became new text.
- **The tapped nav highlight went to the first item, not the drawn one** (e5: drawn on the second row,
  "Funnels", built on "Overview"). Stat cards built number-above-label and dropped the green change until
  a fix. Dropdown "Last 30 days" split into "30 days / Last".
- **Toolbar moved to the bottom jumped to the top** on e5 (moveBar to y 880), covering the heading line.
