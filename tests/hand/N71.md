# n71: repeated items spread as drawn; a table row is not a heading

Replayed the n66 sketches of round 34 e3 (habits: 16 strokes, 12 notes) and e2 (departures: 11 strokes, 9 notes)
word for word: first build only, no fix notes, no note drags.

## Gap (from n66)
- **Habits:** "M T W T F S S, small grey" was typed on a level line drawn 500px wide over the week's columns. The
  build packed the letters and the seven circles in every card 56px apart (target about 80px), layout 49%.
  Two hidden causes found while replaying: single letters are not words to the word reader (it keeps 2+
  letters), so the line said nothing; and the note, typed 5px over its line, also took the card box 29px under
  it ("on these 2 marks"), so the line got no words at all.
- **Departures:** every table row ("14:40 Lisbon TP 1351 B12 Boarding blue pill") was typed on its own long
  line, and n56's "a short phrase on a long line is a heading sized by the line" made each row 60px bold on the
  first build. One fix note brought them back.

## What was built
- **A note typed right on a line is that line's** (`notefocus.ts`): within 8px of a level line, inside its span,
  with no other mark that close and no stacked line next to it, the note binds to the line alone.
- **Short items on a line are spread over it** (`linespread.ts`): three or more items of 1-3 characters (day
  letters, numbers, grades) on a line long enough are told as columns centred evenly from the line's start to its
  end, and anything repeated per item in the rows below (circles, dots) shares those columns. Checked on the page:
  the items' first and last centres, and every full row of like small marks under them.
- **A table row is never a heading:** a line of 4+ short values with 2+ numbers in them, or a line whose note says
  small, is never sized up by its length; table rows are told to stay at body size and checked.
