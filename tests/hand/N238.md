## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **Shapes drawn later were built behind** (e1): a card stack drawn back to front came out with the last-drawn at the
  front of the wrong end: the white front card ended up at the back. One note fixed it, then the words stayed off
  centre until that note named them. Same family as c4's cars. Lead: drawing order is stacking order.
- **A drawn box's height was ignored once a count filled it** (e5): the panel drawn to 820 shrank to 710 and pulled
  the drawn button up with it. "Taller rows" then overshot until the page ran off screen; a second note brought it
  back, with the button now inside the panel. Lead: a drawn box keeps its size, rows share it evenly.
- **Page tap became a fade to a far tap** (e4): the whole page was built dark to orange, the same colour as the
  button tap at the far corner. "Flat dark page, no fade" fixed it first time. Lead: a page tap is one flat colour.
- **A ring with one tap in its body was filled, with the hole taken by the second tap** (e4): the first fix made only
  the quarter arc and dropped the rest of the ring; the second brought it back but in a washed-out brown. Lead: a tap
  on the ring's own body is a slice, inside the hole is the hole; a fix never removes the ring.
- **Short typed text split into a big word and a small one** (e3, e5): "8 to 2" became a big 8 over a small "to 2",
  and survived the fix; "Download now, 412 Mbps" lost Mbps and put the number first. Lead: a bar's label stays one
  line; a stat keeps all its words in typed order.
- **A note's second half was lost or replaced what it sat on** (e4): "Friday was your shortest night, 4 hours 20."
  was bound to the button beside it, became the button's words, and "Set a bedtime" vanished; the "4 hours 20" never
  came back. The status said "on this box" but I let it pass. Lead: a sentence outside a box's edge stays outside.
- **A pill drawn with a tap and no words got invented words** (e5): Online/Offline nobody typed, then right as
  toggles after a note. Lead: a small pill in a row is a toggle unless it has words.
- **A count gave colour only to the first row** (e5): rows 2-6 got blank grey icons; one note listing colours was
  right. Expected, but "icons:" should stay short.
- **A fix aimed at the ring hit its inner dot** (e2): "thick grey ring, no dot" bound to an invented dot and made it a
  grey ring; the ring itself never came. An invented dot in a ring started it: nobody drew one.
- **The hour labels took the pale line's colour** (e3): near-invisible, fixed by a note. Asking for lines at each hour
  gave lines at every half-step, not at the labels.
