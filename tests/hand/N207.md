# n207: a drawn box keeps its drawn height

Found in n151 (round 45 c4, bike repair): on both tries the three service cards, drawn 222px tall, came out about
168px tall and 33px low at the top (n204 had them right). Every check passed anyway.

## Why it happened
- The drawn-box check compared only centres. A card built 54px short and 33px low at the top has its centre just
  6px off, inside the 28px slack, so it "landed". The cards check (n54) only covers boxes with marks drawn inside;
  these cards hold only notes, so nothing ever looked at their height.

## What was built (zero tokens)
- **Drawn height is checked** (`verifyShapeMarks`, `BOX_HEIGHT_PX`): a box drawn 60px or taller (not a ring, card or
  big-word area) whose built height is off by more than 16px or 12% fails, telling its drawn height and top, an exact
  height (not min-height from content), and how much to take out of or add to the gap above it.
- **A row of look-alikes is told once**: boxes in the same row, the same size and short the same way are named on the
  first one ("give every one of them the same height and top"), so the repair does not chase three separate misses.
- Tests: `packages/pipeline/tests/n207-box-height.test.ts` (3).
