# n156: rows typed in a card under its small heading start below that heading, never over it

Found in n155 (round 41 e1 bank home: the transaction rows started at the card's top, on the "Recent" heading,
"Rec" cut off).

## What was found
The rows note ("rows: Corner Grocer −$42.18, Salary +$2,400.00 green, ...") was typed mid-card at y 520, the card
was read as an area of bare rows, and the build put the list at the card's top (first row y 342) with "Recent"
squeezed beside it. Nothing told the build that the short note at the card's top left was a heading the rows go
under. A person types a long rows note wherever there is room.

## What was built
- **Rows under a card heading** (`cardrows.ts`, wired in `intent2.ts` and `run2.ts`): a drawn box at least 280x160
  (not a side panel) with a rows note typed in it (rows of data, or a lead list like "rows: a, b, c; look") and a
  short note (4 words or fewer, no colon) typed in its top 120px above it, with no level line drawn across the card
  under the heading and no other note between. The rows start 18px under the heading; when a small ring or small
  square (12-56px) is drawn at the card's left under the heading, the first row's middle is level with it. Row
  pitch is what fits to the card's foot (40-72px). Works whether the card keeps its notes as typed spots or as an
  area's notes. The build is told the start, pitch and that no row sits beside or over the heading.
- Checked on the page (`card-rows`): the first row's words start under the heading's words and near the start.
  7 unit tests (`n156-card-rows.test.ts`). Zero tokens.
