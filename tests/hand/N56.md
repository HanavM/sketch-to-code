# n56: a long line sets a heading's size, a label drawn under a ring is centred on it, a row of rings keeps its height

Round 33 c4 (herb farm) stayed near 0.5 layout after n55: the heading was built small (28px, its words
running 577px on a 916px line), the labels under the three circles started at each circle's left edge, and
in some builds the whole row of circles sat 24px high.

## What was built
- **A short phrase on a long line is a big heading sized by the line** (`linespan.ts` `lineAsksBig`,
  `spanFontPx`; told in `textplace.ts`): words that would run under half the drawn length at body size, on a
  line 300px or longer, are told before the build as one big line at about the font-size that spans it, and
  the line-span check now covers them too (before, only notes with "big", "heading" etc. were sized).
- **A short line drawn centred under (or just over) a ring or box is that mark's label** (`textplace.ts`
  `columnOf`): told "centred on x" of the mark, checked after the build. Before, measured against the page,
  these lines read as "left" and the build was told to left-align them at the line's start.
- **A row of 3+ boxes or rings all off the same way by 12px or more is one shift** (`shapemark.ts`): each ring
  alone got a 28px slack, so a row 24px high passed; now the row is named with the one spacing to fix.
