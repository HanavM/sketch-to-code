# n120: a big drawn panel is built at its drawn top

Found in n119 (round 38 e3, budget, try 4): the Categories panel was built about 145px below its drawn top and every
check passed, so no repair ran and likeness fell to 30.7.

## Why, and what was built
- **Why it passed:** the drift check only runs on whole-page builds (6+ placed blocks) and fails only when 3+ blocks (or
  a quarter) are off the same way. A budget page can read as 5-6 placed blocks, and one big panel off counts as one
  block, so a panel far off was either not checked at all or outvoted.
- **Big panels are checked on their own** (`drift.ts`, `bigBlockDrift`): any placed block drawn at least ~160px tall
  and ~200px wide (not a full-page backdrop) is matched by its drawn size and x, and its built top must be within 32px
  of its drawn top. It runs on any page with 2+ placed blocks, and also when the whole-page check would pass or call the
  offsets mixed. On a miss the repair is told the panel, its built and drawn top, and where such space usually comes from
  (a lower grid row, wrapper padding, a heading drawn inside it put above it), never with negative margins.
- **Run records kept** (`session.mjs`): each hand run now saves the run records (every check's verdict) as `runs.json`
  beside the digest. Before, they were wiped at the next page, which is why n119's pass could not be explained from
  its files.
