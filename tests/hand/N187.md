## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A note typed under a short line lands on a bigger mark next to it** (8 note moves in 5 pages): stat notes
  under short lines inside a band bound to the band (c2, three times); heading and subtitle notes under lines
  inside a panel bound to the panel (c3, twice); a nav note under the nav line bound to the sun ring below it
  (c4); a logo note bound to the logo and the coloured block under the header (c5); a date note bound to
  the button beside it (c1). Lead: a note's nearest line wins over an enclosing box or a ring further off.
- **A coloured-pen outline with no tap was built filled, and the tapped one hollow** (c3: two yellow outline
  pills and one tapped pill came out inverted; the fix made the outlines plain text with no border at all).
  c5's dark outline button was built as a filled dark pill (fixed by a note). Lead: no tap = outline in the pen
  colour, tap = fill; never swap them, never drop the border.
- **Stats drawn as three short lines across a band were spread to the band edges** (c2: 12 at the far left,
  1921 at the far right; the drawn lines sat about a sixth in). A fix note did not move them.
- **A text line in a dark pen came out white** (c2 logo, drawn brown on cream: white letters; fixed by a note).
- **Shapes given invented 3D detail** (c1 lanterns got caps, ribs and sharp corners; c5 pot became a shaded
  cylinder; c4 noted cloud became a shaded ellipse while the un-noted cloud was right). Same family as n143.
- **A dashed path drawn as a curve came out as thick sausage dashes** (c4); a fix asking for thin dashes did nothing.
- **A lone row line among list rows became a huge heading** (c5: "Open studio" built about 48px bold).
- **Nav wrapped** onto two lines again (c4 "My trips"; one fix put it on one line).
