## Round 35 roll-up (n76 c1-c5 + n77 e1-e5)
Mean likeness **77.9** (c 76.1, e 79.7), median **17.5** strokes, **57.5** words, **8.0** minutes; 9 of 10 pages
needed one fix round. Below the 80 bar and over the 50-word bar: round 35 does not count towards check 1.
Round 34 for comparison: 79.5 mean, 15.5 strokes, 47.5 words, 5.7 min.

Strokes stayed low (13-18 per page, all simple shapes) but **words ran 120-139 on four of five app pages**:
every row of a list or table had to be typed out (titles, dates, amounts). That is the main effort gap now.

## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **No compact way to give a table's or list's rows** (e2, e3, e4, e5): 5-6 rows of data cost 40-90 words
  each page. Leads: (a) a "rows" note that takes data separated by commas or slashes and fills the drawn row
  marks in order, with no direction words needed; (b) paste or type rows in a small grid editor on the
  table mark, counted as data, not notes; (c) draw one row, tick a count (the repeat gesture from n76).
- **A note with `·` separators is printed verbatim as one line** (e4): "Sertraline 50 mg · 1 tablet daily
  · 4 days left" became one bold line, and trailing direction words ("teal Refill", "outline Refill") were
  printed. The fix note split it into name, dose and days correctly. Lead: parts of a note on a card read
  as that card's separate text items (name / detail / side value), and direction words never printed.
- **"big" or "huge" on a number in a drawn box drops the box** (e2, e3): stat cards became bare giant
  numbers (160px) with labels outside the card; one fix put the cards back, numbers still centred, not left.
  Lead: n14's area-box rule fires for any big number; a box with a label and a number is a stat card.
- **A colour tap in a small box inside a big box filled the big one** (e2 red cover filled the whole
  table; e3 a "green dot" note turned the table green). A fix moved the red to the table header only.
  Lead: a tap lands on the smallest mark under it (n53, open).
- **Logo and links on one header line are built centred together** (e4, e5): notes at both ends of the
  header rule bound to the same line, so the header lost its drawn left/right split; the header also grew
  from the drawn 75px to 108px (e4). Lead: notes at the two ends of a line keep their sides (n21 rule for
  one line, not two).
- **Pills group note** (e3): "Week Month Year, dark pills" bound to the first pill and was printed inside it
  verbatim; the third pill was empty. One fix fixed it. Lead: a note listing N words beside N drawn boxes
  gives one word per box.
- **Invented copy** (e5): a "64% of budget spent" caption and a "What was it?" label were added; a fix
  removed the caption.
- **Heading size too small** (e1): "big bold" built about 20px, the drawn line was wider; layout 62.
