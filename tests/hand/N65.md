## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A curve drawn across a filled shape as a ring is read as a divider** (c3): the planet's ring line split
  it into two flat fills (light top, dark bottom) and no ring was drawn. The fix note brought a ring and a
  radial glow but threw away the drawn size and spot (planet shrank to a full circle mid-right, ring flat).
  Lead: a thin open curve that crosses a shape and leaves it on both sides should be a ring/line mark, not
  a split, and a fix must keep the drawn outline.
- **A full-width line with taps on both sides does not split the page** (c5): three page taps made one
  diagonal fade over everything, and the horizon line was built as a white rule. One fix note fixed it
  (sky fade above, solid sea below), but the white rule stayed. Lead: n59's divider split should apply to
  the page itself, and the dividing line should not be left as a rule.
- **A confirmed MOVE fix changed nothing** (c4): the reading was right ("move Fresh daily into the
  circle"), the codemod refused ("into is not a sibling reorder"), the model edit was reverted or empty
  (changedFiles: []), yet verification said "source now inside dest". The badge text stayed in the arch's
  middle. Two bugs: the first build put a note on "2 marks" (arch + circle) at the arch centre, and the
  move verify passed with no change.
- **A note on one line of a stacked giant headline doesn't carry the style to the others** (c1): HOT,
  RAMEN, LATE each got a note; only the last said "giant bold white", and HOT came out as a small red
  eyebrow. The fix made HOT giant but smaller than RAMEN, and the same fix turned NOODLE white (it had
  been black as typed): a fix lost an earlier colour.
- **A line drawn for words inside a filled circle was also built as an underline** (c1, NOODLE).
- **A big outlined number doesn't fill its drawn area box** (c2): 42 built about two thirds of the box.
- **Headings sit below and smaller than their drawn lines** (c2 HILL CLIMB ~25px low, c4 heading wraps
  at 'before' instead of 'the', c5 heading 35px low, overlapping the subtitle).
- **Square boxes come out with rounded corners / pills** (c2 logo, c4 button).
- **The page colour from a tap is still not shown on the sketch** (c1, dark page; known since n49).
