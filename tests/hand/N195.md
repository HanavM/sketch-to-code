# n195: list rows stay rows, and a box drawn with round corners keeps them

Found in n194's pottery replay (round 44 c5): "Saturday mornings, $240 right" and "Open studio, $25 right", each typed
on a ruled list line 480px long, were built as 30-40px bold headings. The line-length rule (n56: few letters on a long
line = a heading sized by the line) fired because the label and price ran under half the line. The first row,
"Tuesday evenings, $240 at right in orange-brown, thin dividers", had enough letters to escape it. The pot, drawn as a
rounded shape, was cleaned to a square path and built as a square block.

## What was built
- **A label and its value is a row.** A note whose copy is a label (five words or fewer, no digits) then, after ", "
  (or a dot or bar), one value (price, number, time, percentage), look words aside, is never sized up by its line.
- **A line ruled in a list is a row.** One of three or more level lines with words, about the same length and left end,
  stacked 18-90px apart, is never sized up by its line.
- Told: each such line is ONE ROW of a list at body size (about 15px), label at the line's left end and value at its
  right end. Checked on the page with the table-row check (any row text over 22px fails, with a repair line).
- **Round corners kept.** A box whose stroke cuts its four corners (median gap over 12px and 6% of the short side) gets
  a corner radius (gap / 0.41, capped at half the short side), and its clean path has arcs, not square corners.

Wider check over the first hand sketch of every page so far (243 pages, 735 level lines with words): only the two
pottery rows change from heading to row (the departures rows it also matches were already body-size table rows).
Round corners: 5 of 668 boxes on 150 pages get a radius, the pot and four lanterns drawn as rounded shapes.
