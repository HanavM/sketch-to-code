## What went wrong (gaps found)
- **A white outline box inside a filled box was built as a filled white card with invented text** (c4 court
  lines): an untapped white-pen box drawn on the green court became a white panel with two made-up blurbs.
  Lead: an untapped box drawn in a pen colour on a filled box is an outline in that colour (court lines,
  window frames), never a card, and never gets text no note gave it.
- **A fix that was read right was thrown away whole** (c4): the fix round read both notes right (court lines
  only; three stat tiles) and edited the page, but the safety check put the whole edit back because one part
  emptied the old stats text. The page did not change at all. Lead: put back only the part that broke, keep
  the rest; and when a note asks to restructure text, emptying the old text is expected, not a break.
- **A count on a drawn box was printed as words** (c4): "x3 stacked: ..." on one stat tile gave one tile with
  the word "stacked:" printed, not three tiles (n201 repeats a box when the count is read; this phrasing
  was missed).
- **Swatch boxes with a note saying "white shadowed cards, name under" lost the white card** (c5): the three
  coloured boxes became the cards themselves, names under them, no white frame. Layout 53.
- **Short nav line noted "bold" came out huge and wrapped** (c3): "Menu Games Find us, bold" built at headline
  size, "Find us" on two lines. Lead: "bold" on a short line keeps its size.
- **Tiles built twice** (c3): each domino had a second black shape behind it (a square box behind the first,
  a clipped one behind the third), and each divider split in two.
- **Body text small and on one line** (c1, c2, c3, c5): the drawn subtitle line wraps to two lines on the
  target; the built text is small and fits on one. Same as round 45.
- **Arch top flattened** (c2): the drawn arch's top was cut flat for about 60px.
