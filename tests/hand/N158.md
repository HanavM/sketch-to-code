# n158: rows typed in open space take the width of the field above them

Found in n157 (round 41 e3 to-do: the rows note typed in open space built half the page wide on one try, 15-18px low on another).

## What was found
The to-do's rows were typed as one note in open space at (640,480), under a 640px "Add a task" field, three filter chips
and a small checkbox drawn at (320,313) for the first row. The note's spot was told as where its words sit ("about y 480,
centred near x 640"), so the build guessed the list's width and start. A person types a long rows note wherever there is
room; the field drawn above says how wide the list is, and the checkbox says where row 1 is.

## What was built
- **Open rows** (`openrows.ts`, wired in `intent2.ts` and `run2.ts`): a note typed where nothing is drawn, with a lead
  and colon that names rows or a list ("checkbox rows, dividers:") and 2-12 items (a lower-case item such as "urgent
  orange right" is a look for the row before it). Its rows take the x span of the nearest drawn box above it at least
  280px wide; they start just under the last thing drawn there, or, when that is a small square or ring at the field's
  left, row 1's middle is level with it. A note typed below (a footer) sets the pitch so the rows end above it.
- The note's typed spot is replaced by the first row's spot, and the build is told the full width, start and pitch
  instead of "sit where typed". Checked on the page (`open-rows`): first row's middle within 26px, its row at least 85%
  of the field's width; a miss names the move. 4 unit tests (`n158-open-rows.test.ts`). Zero tokens.
