The clay remembers every hand that ever doubted it.

At dawn the kiln is still warm from the night firing, and the studio smells of ash and wet earth. Twelve potters share it now.

Each season they choose one glaze and push it as far as it will go. This year it is a red that only appears above 1,280 degrees.
