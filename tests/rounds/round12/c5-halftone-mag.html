The last print shop on Rua Augusta

Four presses, two owners and a cat called Pantone keep ink on paper in a city that went digital.

When Marta opened the shutter in 1987 the street had nine printers. Today the hum of the Heidelberg is the only one left, and it still runs before the cafés open.

Orders come from bands, poets and a ministry that never updated its forms. Nobody here is sentimental about it; the work simply pays.
