THE LONG READISSUE 07 — WINTERSUBSCRIBE

The city that forgot to sleep

At four in the morning the bakeries are already lit, the trams are running empty, and the night shift at the port is handing over to the day. Nobody here agrees on when the day starts. We spent a winter finding out why, riding every last tram and every first ferry, and asking the people who keep the lights on.

“We don't close. We just change who's working.”
