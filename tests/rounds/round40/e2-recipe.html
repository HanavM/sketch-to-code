Lemon ricotta pancakes

25 min · Easy · Breakfast
1
2
4
6

Ingredients

200 g ricotta
2 eggs
120 g flour
1 lemon, zested
150 ml milk
1 tbsp sugar

Method

1Whisk ricotta, eggs, milk and zest until smooth.
2Fold in flour and sugar; rest five minutes.
3Fry spoonfuls in butter, two minutes a side.
