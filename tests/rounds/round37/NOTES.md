Written in one step (2026-10-30, n96) and never sketched in that step. Sketch them in later steps from
`ref/*.png` only: do not open the HTML, targets.json or effects.json while sketching (GOAL.md rules 1 and 4).

Mix: 5 creative landing pages (c1-c5), 5 everyday app pages (e1-e5). Chosen to test the features built since
round 36 (rows from a drawn first row, progress bars, flat panels, headings wrapping onto drawn lines, stat
cards) plus new things: glowing lanterns on a wire, a full-height colour split with three discs, a tilted
stripe behind a huge year and a ringed record, a big disc with a small overlapping badge, a ring round a

a dark sidebar with folder cards over a file table, a four-step progress tracker over a map and item list,
a chat with left and right bubbles and an image message, and a dark dashboard with a seven-bar chart.
